Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. Вставь предлог

1. I learnt the poem _____ heart. → by
2. We met completely _____ chance. → by
3. She did it _____ purpose, I'm sure. → on
4. The lift is _____ order again. → out of
5. He arrived just _____ the nick of time. → in
6. I'm afraid that's _____ the question. → out of
7. Everything is _____ control now. → under
8. She's been feeling _____ the weather all week. → under
9. We'll finish the report _____ time. → in / on
10. He explained it _____ detail. → in
11. I'll be back _____ minutes. → within
12. Let's start again _____ scratch. → from
13. Book the table _____ advance. → in
14. He was arrested and is _____ arrest. → under
15. Please reply _____ writing. → in
16. She's away _____ business until Friday. → on

B. Переведи выражением

1. в двух словах → in a nutshell
2. без предупреждения → without warning
3. в среднем → on average
4. время от времени → from time to time
5. на всякий случай (запас) — «пока что» → for the time being
6. наизусть → by heart
7. напрасно → in vain
8. по ошибке → by mistake
9. с нуля → from scratch
10. в конечном счёте → in the long run
