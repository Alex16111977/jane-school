💡 Challenge: answer in at least 5–8 sentences. Use past tenses, conditionals, and linking words like however / although / as a result.

📋 All B1–B2 Questions

1Can you describe one of your long-term goals and why it is important to you?
2How do you stay motivated and focused on your goals?
3Have you ever faced any obstacles or setbacks while pursuing your goals? How did you overcome them?
4Do you believe in setting specific deadlines for achieving goals, or do you prefer a more flexible approach?
5How do you celebrate when you accomplish a goal?
6Can you share a success story where you achieved a goal that seemed challenging at first?
7Do you believe in the power of visualization and positive affirmations when it comes to achieving goals?
8Have you ever set goals as a group or with a partner? How did it enhance the experience?
9How do you balance pursuing your goals with other aspects of your life, such as family and leisure?
10What advice would you give to someone who is struggling to set and achieve their goals?

📋 All C1–C2 Questions

1How has your understanding of goals and goal-setting evolved over the years?
2Can you discuss a time when you had to adjust or change your goals due to unforeseen circumstances?
3How do you prioritize your goals when you have multiple competing priorities in your life?

5Have you ever helped someone else achieve their goals? How did it feel to be a part of their journey?
6What role do you think self-reflection and self-assessment play in setting and achieving meaningful goals?

8How do you ensure that your goals align with your values and long-term vision for your life?
9Can you discuss any cultural or societal factors that influence how people set and pursue their goals?
10How do you define success in relation to achieving goals? Has your definition changed over time?
