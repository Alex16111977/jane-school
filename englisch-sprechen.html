🎯 Rules of the challenge

  1. Click New topic → read it out loud
  2. Click Start 60 sec → talk non-stop until the timer ends
  3. Don't stop. Don't translate. Don't worry about grammar.
  4. If you blank — say "umm, what I mean is..." and keep going
  5. Record yourself the first try & the third try — compare!

💬 Read both sides aloud — YOU = blue, THEM = orange. Then close your eyes and improvise the same conversation in your own words.

📱 1. Texting in person — "Did you see her post?"
Two friends checking phones at a café.
THEMWait — did you see Anna's new post?
YOUNo way — what did she post?
THEMShe literally moved to Berlin without telling anyone!
YOUHold on, let me check... oh wow, that's huge. Are you serious?
THEMYeah, I literally just saw it. Should we text her?
YOUDefinitely. I'll send her a voice message right now.
💡 Catch the native vibes: "No way", "hold on", "literally", "Are you serious?"
😭 2. Complaining about a grade
After school, walking home with your best friend.
YOUDude, I cannot believe I got a C on the history test.
THEMWait, didn't you study for like a whole week?
YOUI did! That's what's so frustrating — I knew all the dates by heart.
THEMYeah, but the essay part is tricky. He always grades it super strict.
YOUHonestly, I'm thinking of talking to him after class.
THEMGo for it. The worst he can say is no.
💡 Catch: "Dude", "I cannot believe", "for like", "Go for it", "The worst he can say is no".
🎬 3. Planning a Netflix night
Saturday afternoon, video call with a friend.
THEMSo, my place tonight? Movie night?
YOUYeah, totally! What are we watching?
THEMI'm kinda into the new Netflix thriller. Have you seen the trailer?
YOULooks creepy but cool. I'm in. Should I bring snacks?
THEMJust popcorn — I'll handle the drinks. 8pm work for you?
YOUSounds perfect. See you then!
💡 Catch: "Yeah, totally", "kinda", "I'm in", "I'll handle...", "8pm work for you?"
🎵 4. Recommending a song
In the school hallway, between classes.
YOUHave you heard that new song by — you know — the British band?
THEMWhich one? Be more specific!
YOUThe one with the slow guitar intro — it goes super hard at the end.
THEMNo idea what you're talking about. Just send me the link!
YOUWill do. Trust me, you'll love it. It's been stuck in my head for days.
THEMAlright, send it now — I'm bored in chemistry.
💡 Catch: "you know" as filler, "goes super hard", "No idea what you're talking about", "stuck in my head".
🤔 5. Making excuses
Your friend invited you to a party. You don't really want to go.
THEMSo, you coming to Mike's party Saturday or what?
YOUUgh, I'd love to, but honestly, I'm super behind on homework.
THEMCome on, just an hour or two!
YOUI know, but my mom's been on my case lately. Maybe next time?
THEMFine, fine. But you owe me one!
YOUDeal! I promise I'll come to the next one.
💡 Catch: "or what", "Ugh", "behind on...", "on my case", "you owe me one", "Deal!"
📚 6. Asking for help with homework
Texting your smart classmate at 11pm.
YOUHey, are you still up? I'm completely lost on the math homework.
THEMYeah, I'm awake. Which question?
YOUNumber 7 — the one about percentages. I've been staring at it for 20 minutes.
THEMOh, that one's actually easy once you see the trick. Take a screenshot and I'll explain.
YOUYou're a lifesaver. Seriously.
THEMNo worries — you helped me with English last week!
💡 Catch: "still up", "completely lost", "You're a lifesaver", "No worries".

Story Starters — finish the tale

2-min stories

Read the opening line aloud — then improvise the next 2 minutes of the story.

"It started as a perfectly normal Tuesday, but then I got a text from a number I didn't recognize..."
Use past tenses · build suspense · describe how you felt
"I'll never forget the day I missed my flight by three minutes..."
Sequence words: first, then, suddenly, in the end
"My grandma sat me down and said, 'You won't believe what happened to me when I was your age.'"
Use reported speech: "She told me that..." / "She said..."
"It was 2am, I was alone in the apartment, and that's when I heard it — a sound coming from the kitchen..."
Past Continuous + Past Simple: "I was sleeping when..."
"If I told you I once met a celebrity in the most embarrassing way possible, would you believe me?"
Conditional + storytelling · build the embarrassment slowly
⚖️

Debate yourself — agree or disagree?

Pick a side + 3 reasons

Read the statement · choose YES or NO · give 3 reasons in 90 seconds · then try the opposite side!

Social media does more harm than good for teenagers.
Use: "Firstly... Secondly... Lastly..." + "On the other hand..."
Schools should ban grades and only give feedback.
Use: "I strongly believe..." / "I'm not convinced because..."
Everyone should be required to learn a second language.
Modal verbs: should, must, would, could
It's better to travel alone than with friends.
Comparatives: better than, more...than, the most...
Working from home is the future for everyone.
Use future forms: will, is going to, might
Books are better than movies.
Personal preference + reasons + examples
🖼️

Imagination prompts — describe the scene

Close your eyes & talk

Close your eyes · picture the scene · describe everything you "see" out loud for 90 seconds.

A busy coffee shop on a rainy Sunday morning — you walk in, look around, and describe what you see, hear, and smell.
Your childhood bedroom — describe the furniture, the colors, the smell, your favorite spot.
A beach at sunset in summer — describe the people, the light, the sounds, what's happening around you.
The first day at a new school — describe the building, the people, your feelings during the first hour.
A traditional family dinner in your country — describe the table, the food, who's there, what people are saying.

🎵 Drill these out loud, slowly first, then faster. Record yourself, then a native speaker (YouTube), then compare. This is how accents get fixed.

👅 The TH trap

/θ/ & /ð/

Tip: stick the tip of your tongue between your teeth, then blow air. Don't say "z" or "s" or "f"!

Thirty thousand thirsty thieves.
voiceless θ
This, that, these, those — they are there.
voiced ð
I think that the thing in that three-storey house is thick.
mix both

💨 W vs. V — the German trap

/w/ vs. /v/

W = round your lips like saying "oo". V = touch your top teeth to your bottom lip.

Very well, Wendy.
classic mix-up
We were very, very worried.
rapid alternation
Victor went to Vienna and waved at William.
names

🎯 Past -ED endings

/t/ /d/ /ɪd/

Three pronunciations: /t/ after voiceless (walked) · /d/ after voiced (lived) · /ɪd/ after t/d (wanted)

I walked, talked, worked, watched.
all /t/
She lived, loved, played, called.
all /d/
They wanted, needed, decided, started.
all /ɪd/

🎤 Sound natural — word linking

gonna / wanna / gotta

Native speakers blend words together. Don't say each word separately!

"I am going to..." → "I'm gonna..."
speech blend
"I want to..." → "I wanna..."
speech blend
"I have got to..." → "I gotta..."
speech blend
"What are you doing?" → "Whatcha doin'?"
very casual
"Did you eat yet?" → "D'jeet yet?"
super native!

💬 10 native phrases that make you sound fluent

use them today
"You know what I mean?"
Asking if listener follows you
"...so the whole thing was just weird, you know what I mean?"
"Long story short..."
Skip to the point
"Long story short, we missed the train."
"To be fair..."
Acknowledge the other side
"To be fair, she did warn us."
"Now that you mention it..."
React to what someone said
"Now that you mention it, I noticed the same thing!"
"It is what it is."
Accept reality
"We lost the match. It is what it is."
"I'm not gonna lie..."
About to say something honest
"I'm not gonna lie, I'm exhausted."
"My bad!"
Casual apology / "my fault"
"Oh, my bad — I didn't see you there."
"Pretty much."
Yes, basically / almost
"You're done? — Pretty much."
"For real?"
Really? / Are you serious?
"She's moving to Tokyo? For real?"
"That makes sense."
I understand / logical
"...so that's why she was late. — That makes sense."

🌟 The fluency formula

10 min daily > 1 hour weekly. Pick ONE mode per day: Mon — 60-sec challenge x3 · Tue — 2 conversations (read + improvise) · Wed — 1 story starter (2-min talk) · Thu — 1 debate (both sides!) · Fri — pronunciation drills + 5 native phrases · Sat/Sun — describe a scene + record yourself.
