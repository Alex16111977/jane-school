a friend of mine · my own · there vs it

Как сказать «мой собственный», «один, сам по себе» — и когда there, а когда it

🔑

a friend of mine · my own room

Две конструкции, которых нет в русском

a friend of mine = один из моих друзей. Русское «мой друг» без контекста звучит как «тот самый», поэтому англичане уточняют.

a friend of mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs
of mineA friend of mine is getting married on Saturday. не a friend of me
of oursWe went on holiday with some friends of ours. не of us
of hisHarry had an argument with a neighbour of his.
of yoursIt was a good idea of yours to go to the cinema.
с именем — 'sThat woman is a friend of my sister's. · It was a good idea of Tom's.
of mineI borrowed the car from a colleague of mine.
of hersShe showed me some photos of hers from the trip.
of theirsWe stayed with friends of theirs in Kraków.

🏠 my own — «свой собственный»

my / your / his / her / our / their own + существительное

Значит: только мой, не общий и не взятый у кого-то.

my own roomI don't want to share. I want my own room.
their own houseVicky and Gary would like to have their own house.
its ownIt's a shame the flat hasn't got its own parking space.
my own faultIt's my own fault that I have no money.
без существительногоWhy do you want my car? Why don't you use your own?
my ownI finally have my own desk at the office.
your ownBring your own towel — they don't provide them.
their ownThe kids each have their own room now.
of my ownShe'd love a flat of her own.
❌an own house — артикль с own не ставят

💡 Второй вариант той же мысли: a room of my own, a house of your own:
I'd like to have a room of my own. · He has too many problems of his own.

✂️ own = «сам, а не кто-то за меня»

cuts his own hairPaul usually cuts his own hair. = стрижёт себя сам
grow my ownI'd like a garden so I could grow my own vegetables.
make your ownDo you bake your own bread?

⚖️ Сравни с have something done: He cuts his own hair (сам) — He has his hair cut (его стрижёт мастер).

🚶

on my own / by myself

Два способа сказать «сам / один»

on + притяжательное + own
  • on my own
  • on your own
  • on his / her / its own
  • on our / their own
by + возвратное
  • by myself / yourself
  • by himself / herself / itself
  • by ourselves / yourselves
  • by themselves

Оба значат alone — выбирай любое.

📝 Примеры

жить одномуI like living on my own. = I like living by myself.
предпочитаютSome people prefer to live on their own / by themselves.
сидел одинJack was sitting on his own in a corner of the cafe.
вопросDid you go on holiday on your own?
сам справилсяShe built the whole website by herself.

📌 Слово alone тоже верно, но чуть суше: I was alone at home. А lonely — уже про чувство: «одиноко».

🪞

myself — когда для усиления, а когда не нужно вовсе

Дополнение к странице Pronouns

💪 myself = «сам, лично»

в конце фразы«Who repaired your bike?» — «I repaired it myself.»
упрёкI'm not doing your work for you. You can do it yourself.
предложениеLet's paint the house ourselves — it'll be much cheaper.
про вещьThe film itself wasn't very good, but I loved the music.
после подлежащегоLisa herself doesn't think so. = Lisa doesn't think so herself.

⚠️ Глаголы, после которых myself НЕ нужен

❌
  • I can't relax myself.
  • You need to concentrate yourself.
  • What time shall we meet ourselves?
  • I feel myself nervous.
  • He washed himself and dressed himself.
✅
  • I can't relax.
  • You need to concentrate.
  • What time shall we meet?
  • I feel nervous.
  • He got up, washed, shaved and dressed. (или got dressed)

Список: feel, relax, concentrate, meet, wash, shave, dress, hurry, change — в английском они уже «возвратные» сами по себе.

🔄 -selves или each other?

themselves = каждый на себяKate and Joe looked at themselves in the mirror.оба смотрели на своё отражение
each other = друг на другаKate and Joe looked at each other.Кейт на Джо, Джо на Кейт

one another = each other: How long have you known each other / one another?

🔎

there … или it …

Первое упоминание — there. Уже знакомое — it.

there — сообщаю, что нечто существуетThere's a new restaurant in Hill Street.
it — про уже названноеWe went to the new restaurant. It's very good.
❌ частая ошибкаIt was a lot of traffic. → There was a lot of traffic.
there = появлениеThere has been a big rise in the cost of living.
there isThere's a good bakery two streets away.
there wasThere was nobody at the reception desk.
there areThere are three buses an hour on Sundays.
it isIt's too far to walk — let's take a taxi.
it wasIt was so foggy that we couldn't see the road.
it = конкретная ситуацияI wasn't expecting her call. It was a complete surprise.
оба сразуI like this town. There's a lot to do here. It's an interesting place.
there = «там»The house is empty. There's nobody living there.

🧱 there + модальные и обороты

there will bethere might bethere must be there should have beenthere would have been there is sure to bethere is bound to be there is likely to bethere is going to bethere used to be
might be«Is there a flight tonight?» — «There might be. I'll check.»
wouldn't beIf people drove more carefully, there wouldn't be so many accidents.
must have beenI heard music. There must have been somebody at home.
bound to beThere's bound to be a cafe near here. = sure to be
used to beThere used to be a cinema here, but it closed a few years ago.
there — есть шумThey live on a busy road. There must be a lot of noise.
it — о самой ситуацииThey live on a busy road. It must be very noisy.

📌 It … в начале предложения

Английский не любит длинное подлежащее в начале — вместо него ставят it:

it's + прилагательное + toIt's dangerous to walk in the road. не To walk in the road is dangerous
it takesIt didn't take us long to get here.
it's a shame thatIt's a shame (that) you can't come.
it's not worthIt's not worth waiting any longer. Let's go.
расстояниеHow far is it from here to the airport?
времяIt's a long time since we last saw you.
погодаIt was windy. — но There was a cold wind. чувствуешь разницу?

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. Переведи

1. Один мой друг работает в банке. → A friend of mine works in a bank.
2. Это была твоя идея. → It was an idea of yours. / It was your idea.
3. У меня наконец-то своя комната. → I finally have my own room. / a room of my own.
4. Он сам себе стрижёт волосы. → He cuts his own hair.
5. Я живу один. → I live on my own. / I live by myself.
6. Она сделала это сама. → She did it herself. / She did it by herself.
7. Это моя собственная вина. → It's my own fault.

B. there или it?

1. _____ was a lot of traffic this morning. → There
2. _____ was very cold yesterday. → It
3. _____ 's a new café on the corner. _____ 's really nice. → There … It
4. _____ used to be a school here. → There
5. _____ is a long way from Kyiv to Lisbon. → It
6. _____ must have been an accident — the road is closed. → There
7. _____ 's no point in arguing. → There
8. _____ 's not worth arguing about this. → It

C. Исправь ошибку

1. He is a friend of me. → a friend of mine
2. They want to buy an own flat. → their own flat
3. I did it by my own. → on my own / by myself
4. I can't relax myself after work. → I can't relax after work.
5. It was a lot of people at the party. → There were a lot of people…
6. To learn languages is useful. → It's useful to learn languages.
7. We met ourselves at six. → We met at six.
