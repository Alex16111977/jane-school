Phrasal Verbs по частицам

Не зубрить списком, а понять логику частиц: in/out · on/off · up/down · away/back — 150+ глаголов с переводом

🧩

Как устроен phrasal verb

Глагол + маленькое слово = новый смысл

Частиц немного, и они повторяются: in, on, up, away, by, about, over, round/around, out, off, down, back, through, along, forward.
Иногда смысл прямой (get on = сесть в транспорт), иногда полностью новый (break down = сломаться).

прямое значениеThe bus was full. We couldn't get on. · A woman got into the car and drove off.
новое значениеSorry I'm late — the car broke down. заглохла
новое значениеI never found out who sent the flowers. так и не узнал
новое значениеI was nervous as the plane took off. взлетал
новое значениеIn the end I gave up. сдался
новое значениеHow did you get on in the exam? как всё прошло
новое значениеMy French is enough to get by. чтобы кое-как справляться

⭐ Главное правило: куда ставить дополнение

✅ Существительное — можно и так, и так
  • I turned on the light.
  • I turned the light on.
  • I'm going to take off my shoes / take my shoes off.
  • Don't wake up the baby / wake the baby up.
⚠️ Местоимение — только в середину
  • I turned it on.  turned on it
  • I'm going to take them off.  take off them
  • Don't wake her up.  wake up her
  • Don't throw it away.  throw away it

🔑 Короткое it / them / him / her / me всегда проскакивает вперёд частицы. Это и есть главный экзаменационный вопрос по теме.

🔗 Phrasal verb + ещё один предлог

look up atWe looked up at the plane as it flew above us.
run away fromWhy did you run away from me?
keep up withYou're walking too fast — I can't keep up with you.
look forward toAre you looking forward to your trip?
get on withHe gets on with everyone at work.
put up withWe have to put up with a lot of noise.
come up withShe's always coming up with new ideas.
cut down onI'm trying to cut down on sugar.

У таких трёхчастных глаголов дополнение всегда идёт в конец: I can't put up with it. (не put it up with)

in / out

Внутрь — наружу, и всё, что из этого выросло

↔️ Прямая пара

in = внутрьHow did the thieves get in?залезть внутрь
out = наружуStay in the car. Don't get out.
let yourself inHere's a key, you can let yourself in.
be locked outI had no key, so I was locked out.
move inI'm moving in on Friday.но: moving into my new flat
move outThey moved out last week.
check inI got to the airport and checked in.
check outWe paid the bill and checked out.
dive inShe walked to the edge and dived in.
climb out (of)She swam and then climbed out (of the pool).

📥 Другие глаголы + IN

drop inзайти без предупреждения — I dropped in to see Chris on my way home.
join inприсоединиться к тому, что уже идёт — They were playing cards, so I joined in.
plug inвключить в розетку — The fridge isn't working — you haven't plugged it in.
take sb inобмануть — He said he was a policeman and I was completely taken in.
fill in / fill outзаполнить бланк — Please fill in the application form.
hand inсдать работу — Hand in your essays by Friday.
give inуступить, сдаться — He argued for hours before finally giving in.

📤 Другие глаголы + OUT

eat outпоесть в кафе — There was no food at home, so we decided to eat out.
drop out (of)бросить учёбу/гонку — Gary went to university but dropped out after a year.
get out ofотвертеться — I promised I'd go, and now I can't get out of it.
leave outпропустить, не включить — You can leave out the word «that».
cross outзачеркнуть — Some names on the list had been crossed out.

🔆 out = «погас, не горит»

go outSuddenly all the lights went out.
put outпотушить — I put the fire out with an extinguisher.
turn outвыключить свет — I turned the lights out before leaving.
blow outзадуть — You can blow out the candle.

💪 work out — четыре разных смысла

заниматься спортомRachel works out at the gym three times a week.
складываться, получатьсяI hope everything works out well for you. · Things didn't work out.
выходить (о сумме)The bill works out at £32.45 each.
посчитать / разобратьсяI can't work it out in my head. · They're trying to work out what caused the accident. = figure out

🔎 Ещё out — самые нужные

carry outвыполнить (приказ, план, исследование) — An investigation will be carried out.
find outвыяснить — The police never found out who did it.
hand / give outраздать — The speaker gave out information sheets.
point outуказать, обратить внимание — I didn't realise I'd made a mistake until somebody pointed it out.
run out (of)закончиться — We ran out of petrol on the motorway.
sort outуладить, разобрать — There are a few problems we need to sort out.
turn outоказаться — He turned out to be right. · It turned out that they'd never met.
try outиспытать — The company is trying out new software.

on / off

Включено / выключено · надеть / снять · продолжать / уехать

💡 Техника: on = включено, off = выключено

turn / switch on/offShall I leave the lights on or turn them off?
be on«Is the heating on?» — «No, I switched it off.»
put onLet's put some music on. · I'll put the kettle on.

👟 Одежда и вес

put onнадеть — My hands were cold, so I put my gloves on.
take offснять — It was warm, so I took off my coat.
try onпримерить — I tried on a jacket, but it didn't look right.
put on weightпоправиться — I've put on two kilos this month.

📅 События

go onпроисходить — What's all that noise? What's going on?
call offотменить — The concert had to be called off because of the weather.
put offотложить — The election has been put off until January. · We can't put off making a decision.
go offсработать (о будильнике, сигнализации) — Did you hear the alarm go off?

🚗 off = прочь, в путь

be off (to)Tomorrow I'm off to Paris. = уезжаю
drive / ride / walk / run offAnna got on her bike and rode off.
go off (to)Mark left home at eighteen and went off to Canada.
set offотправиться в путь — We set off early to avoid the traffic.
take offвзлететь — After a long delay, the plane finally took off.
see sb offпроводить — We went to the station to see her off.

➡️ on = продолжать

go onThe party went on until 4 in the morning.
go on / carry on doingWe can't go on spending money like this. · I don't want to carry on working here.
carry on withPlease carry on with what you're doing.
keep on doingHe keeps on criticising me! = keeps criticising
drive / walk / play onShall we stop here or drive on to the next station?

🤝 get on — три значения

как дела / как идётHow are you getting on in your new job?
ладитьJoanne and Karen don't get on. · Richard gets on well with his neighbours.
взяться за делоI must get on with my work. I have a lot to do.

😴 Ещё off — очень разговорные

doze / drop / nod offзадремать — I dozed off during the lecture.
finish offдоделать — I'll finish it off tomorrow.
put sb offотбить желание — We were put off by the long queue. · What put you off applying?
rip sb offобдирать, обманывать — £2,000 for that? I think you were ripped off.
show offвыпендриваться — Look at that boy riding with no hands — he's just showing off.
tell sb offотчитать — Clare's mother told her off for wearing dirty shoes.

up / down

Вверх-вниз, громче-тише, больше-меньше

↔️ Зеркальные пары

put up (на стену)I put a picture up on the wall.
take downI didn't like it, so I took it down.
pick upThere was a letter on the floor. I picked it up.
put downI stopped writing and put down my pen.
stand upAlan stood up and walked out.
sit / bend / lie downI bent down to tie my shoelace.
turn up (громче)I can't hear the TV. Can you turn it up?
turn down (тише/слабее)The oven is too hot — turn it down.

🏗️ down = разрушить, снести

knock downSome old houses were knocked down to build the shopping centre.
be knocked downA man was knocked down by a car. сбила машина
cut downWhy did you cut down the tree in your garden?
blow downSeveral trees were blown down in the storm.
burn downThey put out the fire before the house burnt down.

🔽 down = становится меньше

slow downYou're driving too fast. Slow down.
calm downCalm down — there's no point getting angry.
cut down (on)I'm trying to cut down on coffee.

🔧 Другие глаголы + DOWN

break downсломаться / развалиться — The car broke down. · Their marriage broke down.
close / shut downзакрыться (о бизнесе) — The shop closed down a few years ago.
let sb downподвести — You can rely on Paul. He'll never let you down.
turn sb/sth downотклонить — I applied for several jobs but was turned down. · She decided to turn the job down.
write downзаписать — I wrote it down, but I can't find it now.

🏃 up = подойти, догнать

come / go / walk up (to)A man came up to me in the street and asked for money.
catch up (with)You go on and I'll catch up with you / catch you up.
keep up (with)You're walking too fast — I can't keep up. · You're doing well. Keep it up!

🚀 up = начать, запустить

set upосновать, создать — The government has set up a committee. · She set up her own business.
take upзаняться (хобби) — Laura took up photography a few years ago.
fix upорганизовать — We've fixed up a meeting for Monday.
grow upвырасти — Amy was born in Hong Kong but grew up in Australia.
bring upрастить ребёнка — She was brought up by her grandparents.

🧹 up = до конца, полностью

clean / clear / tidy upLook at this mess! Who's going to tidy up?
wash upмыть посуду — I hate washing up. = doing the washing-up
use upизрасходовать — We have a lot of vegetables and I want to use them up.
eat up / drink upдоесть, допить — Eat up your dinner!
give upбросить, сдаться — Don't give up! · She got bored with her job and gave it up.
end upоказаться в итоге — Three men ended up in hospital. · I ended up sleeping on a bench.
make upсоставлять — Children under 16 make up half the population. · Air is made up of nitrogen and oxygen.
take up (space/time)занимать — Most of the room was taken up by a large table.
turn up / show upпоявиться, прийти — We arranged to meet, but he didn't turn up.

up — ещё значения

Разговор, настроение, разрушение, отношения

💬 up в разговоре и идеях

bring upподнять тему — Please don't bring it up again.
come upвсплыть в разговоре — Some interesting things came up in our discussion.
come up withпридумать — She's always coming up with new ideas.
make upвыдумать — What he told you wasn't true. He made it all up.
look upпосмотреть в словаре — If you don't know the word, look it up.
speak upговорить громче — Could you speak up? I can't hear you.

😊 Настроение и деньги

cheer upвзбодриться / подбодрить — You look sad. Cheer up! · What can we do to cheer her up?
save up (for)копить — Dan is saving up for a trip to New Zealand.
clear upпроясниться (о погоде) — It was raining, but it cleared up later.

💥 up = разрушить

blow upвзорваться / взорвать — The engine caught fire and blew up. · The bridge was blown up.
tear upпорвать на куски — I didn't read the letter — I just tore it up.
beat sb upизбить — A friend of mine was attacked and beaten up.
break up / split up (with)расстаться — Kate and Paul have split up.

🧰 Полезные разные

do up (одежда)застегнуть — It's cold. Do up your coat.
do up (дом)отремонтировать — The kitchen looks great now it's been done up.
put up withтерпеть — We have to put up with a lot of noise from the traffic.
hold upзадерживать — I don't want to hold you up. · The plans have been held up.
mix upперепутать — The brothers look similar — people often mix them up / get them mixed up.

away / back

Прочь — и обратно

↔️ Прямая пара

away = прочь / из домаWe're going away on holiday today.
back = обратноWe'll be back in three weeks.
drive awayShe started the engine and drove away.
walk backAfter the meal, we walked back to our hotel.
fly / blow awayThe bird flew away. · The ticket blew away in the wind.
give back / put backI forgot to give them back. · Can you put it back on the shelf?

🏃 Другие глаголы + AWAY

get awayсбежать — We tried to catch the thief, but she got away.
get away withвыйти сухим из воды — I parked illegally but got away with it.
keep away (from)не подходить — Keep away from the edge of the pool.
give awayотдать даром — «Did you sell your bike?» — «No, I gave it away.»

throw awayвыбросить — I kept the letter but threw away the envelope.

↩️ Другие глаголы + BACK

wave / smile / shout backв ответ — I waved to her and she waved back.

call / phone / ring backперезвонить — I can't talk now. I'll call you back in ten minutes.

pay backвернуть деньги — If you borrow money, you have to pay it back. · I'll pay you back next week.
take backвернуть в магазин / взять слова назад — I took the shirt back because it didn't fit.

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. Подставь частицу: in, out, on, off, up, down, away, back

1. The car broke _____ on the motorway. → down
2. The plane took _____ two hours late. → off
3. We've run _____ of milk. → out
4. Don't give _____ — keep trying! → up
5. The concert was called _____ because of the storm. → off
6. I'll call you _____ in five minutes. → back
7. She was brought _____ by her grandparents. → up
8. Please fill _____ this form. → in / out
9. Throw that old bread _____. → away
10. Can you turn the music _____? It's too loud. → down
11. He didn't turn _____ to the meeting. → up
12. I get _____ well with my flatmates. → on

B. Переставь предложение с местоимением

1. I turned on the light. → I turned it on.
2. Don't wake up the baby. → Don't wake her/him up.
3. Take off your shoes. → Take them off.
4. She gave away her old clothes. → She gave them away.
5. Look up the word. → Look it up.
6. I can't put up with the noise. → I can't put up with it. (трёхчастный — ничего не переставляем)

C. Замени выделенное фразовым глаголом

1. I discovered the truth by accident. → found out
2. They cancelled the match. → called off
3. Don't delay your decision. → put off
4. He started a new company last year. → set up
5. She invented the whole story. → made it up
6. We must tolerate the noise. → put up with
7. They rejected my application. → turned it down
8. I escaped without paying a fine. → got away with it
9. She disappointed us again. → let us down
10. I need to reduce my coffee intake. → cut down on coffee
