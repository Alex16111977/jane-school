Придаточные с who / that / which

Когда местоимение можно выбросить · запятые меняют смысл · whose / whom / where · the man talking to Tom

🔗

who · that · which

Придаточное отвечает на вопрос «какой именно?»

Зачем это нужно: два предложения склеиваются в одно, а местоимение he / she / it / they заменяется на who / that / which.

два предложенияI met a Canadian woman. She is an English teacher.
одно предложениеI met a Canadian woman who is an English teacher.

❌ Ошибка новичка: оставить оба. Where are the keys that they were on the table? → ✅ Where are the keys that were on the table?

👤 Кто и что

ЛюдиВещи
основной вариантwhothat (чаще) / which
тоже можноthat—
нельзяwhichwho
who + людиThe woman who lives next door is a doctor.
who + людиI don't like people who complain all the time.
определениеAn architect is someone who designs buildings.
that / which + вещиI don't like stories that have unhappy endings.
that / which + вещиGrace works for a company which makes furniture.
that + люди тоже окThe woman that lives next door is a doctor.

⚠️ what ≠ that — самая частая ошибка славян

what = «то, что» и само по себе заменяет существительное. После существительного идёт только that / which / who.

✅ what = the thing thatWhat happened was my fault.= то, что случилось
❌ после существительногоEverything what happened… → Everything that happened was my fault.
❌The machine what broke down… → The machine that broke down is working again.
❌all the money what I had → all the money (that) I had

Проверка: если перед пропуском стоит слово-предмет (everything, the machine, the money) — that. Если ничего не стоит — what.

✂️

Когда who/that/which можно выбросить

Один вопрос решает всё: подлежащее это или дополнение

🔒 Подлежащее — выбросить НЕЛЬЗЯ
  • The woman who lives next door… (она живёт)
  • the keys that were on the table (ключи лежали)
  • Признак: сразу за who/that идёт глагол.
✂️ Дополнение — выбросить МОЖНО
  • The woman (who) I wanted to see… (я хотел увидеть её)
  • the keys (that) you lost
  • Признак: за who/that идёт новое подлежащее (I, you, Lisa).

📝 Живые примеры без местоимения

без thatDid you find the keys you lost?
без thatThe dress Lisa bought doesn't fit her.
без thatIs there anything I can do?
без thatThe film we watched last night was terrible.
❌the keys you lost them · the dress Lisa bought it — второй раз предмет не называют!

🗣️ В разговорной речи местоимение выбрасывают почти всегда, когда это возможно. Звучит естественнее.

📍 Куда девается предлог

В обычной речи предлог остаётся в конце придаточного:

toDo you know the woman Tom is talking to?
inThe bed I slept in wasn't comfortable.
forAre these the books you were looking for?
next toThe man I was sitting next to talked all the time.
❌the books you were looking for them · the man I was sitting next to him

Книжный вариант — предлог впереди: the woman to whom Tom is talking, the bed in which I slept. В разговоре так почти не говорят.

🗝️

whose · whom · where

Чей · кого · где

🔑 whose = «чей»

whose заменяет his / her / their
their carWe helped some people whose car had broken down.
her husbandA widow is a woman whose husband is dead.
his/her brotherI met someone whose brother I went to school with.
who — он самI met a man who knows you.он тебя знает
whose — его сестраI met a man whose sister knows you.его сестра тебя знает

⚠️ whose и who's звучат одинаково, но who's = who is / who has:
a friend who's learning Arabic (= who is) · a friend who's just started (= who has) · a friend whose sister is learning Arabic.

🎩 whom — формальный вариант who

как дополнениеGeorge is a person whom I admire very much.
предлог + whomIt's important to have friends with whom you can relax.
в жизни говорятa person I admire a lot · friends you can relax with

📌 whom обязателен только сразу после предлога (to whom, with whom). Во всех остальных случаях — книжно; в речи используй who или вообще ничего.

📍 where — о месте

whereI went back to the town where I grew up.
whereThe restaurant where we had lunch was near the airport.
whereI'd like to live in a place where there is plenty of sunshine.

🕓 the day / the time / the reason — вообще без слова-связки

the dayFriday? That's the day I'm going away.
the last timeThe last time I saw her, she looked great.
the year2005 was the year I was born.
the reasonThe reason I'm calling is to ask your advice. = the reason that / why I'm calling

Можно добавить that (или why после reason), но без них естественнее.

✏️

Два типа придаточных — запятая решает всё

Defining (без запятых) vs non-defining (с запятыми)

Тип 1 — без запятых
  • Отвечает: какой именно?
  • The woman who lives next door is a doctor.
  • Grace works for a company that makes furniture.
  • We stayed at the hotel (that) you recommended.
  • Без него непонятно, о ком речь.
Тип 2 — с запятыми
  • Даёт дополнительную информацию.
  • My brother Ben, who lives in Hong Kong, is an architect.
  • Anna told me about her new job, which she's enjoying a lot.
  • We stayed at the Park Hotel, which a friend recommended.
  • Уже и так ясно, о ком речь.

📊 Что можно и нельзя в каждом типе

Тип 1 (без запятых)Тип 2 (с запятыми)
that✅ можно❌ нельзя
выбросить who/which✅ можно (если дополнение)❌ нельзя никогда
whomредко✅ нормально
whose / where✅✅
Тип 2 — только whoJohn, who speaks French and Italian, works as a guide. не John, that speaks
Тип 2 — нельзя выброситьThis morning I met Chris, who I hadn't seen for ages.
Тип 2 — whoseLisa, whose car had broken down, was in a bad mood.
Тип 2 — whereKate has just been to Sweden, where her daughter lives.

🧠 Запятая меняет смысл

без запятыхMy sister who lives in Berlin is a nurse.у меня несколько сестёр — речь о берлинской
с запятымиMy sister, who lives in Berlin, is a nurse.сестра одна, и кстати живёт в Берлине

🎩 preposition + whom / which (книжный стиль)

to whomMr Lee, to whom I spoke at the meeting, is interested.
without whichWe had a good map, without which we would have got lost.
разговорный вариантMr Lee, who I spoke to at the meeting… тогда whom не используем
которого раньше не слышалShe works for a company called Latoma, which I'd never heard of.

🔢 all of whom / most of which

людиHelen has three brothers, all of whom are married.
вещиThey asked me a lot of questions, most of which I couldn't answer.
none ofMartin tried on three jackets, none of which fitted him.
neither ofTwo men, neither of whom I had seen before, came in.
many ofSue has a lot of friends, many of whom she was at school with.
the name of whichWe stayed at a hotel, the name of which I don't remember.

⭐ which про ВСЮ ситуацию (не what!)

, whichJoe got the job, which surprised everybody. = тот факт, что он получил работу
, whichSarah couldn't meet us, which was a shame. не what was a shame
, whichThe weather was good, which we hadn't expected.

Здесь which = «что» о целом предложении. Всегда с запятой и никогда через what.

✂️

-ing и -ed clauses

Придаточное без who/which — короче и естественнее

полная формаWho is the woman who is talking to Tom?
короткая — так говорят чащеWho is the woman talking to Tom?

Правило: выбрасываем who/which + be. Активное действие → -ing, пассивное → -ed / V3.

🏃 -ing = сам делает

в момент речиPolice investigating the crime are looking for three men.
тогдаWho were those people waiting outside?
тогдаI was woken up by a bell ringing.
постоянноThe road connecting the two villages is very narrow.
постоянноI have a large room overlooking the garden.
постоянноCan you think of a flower beginning with T?

🛡️ -ed / V3 = с ним это сделали

injuredThe boy injured in the accident was taken to hospital.
paintedGeorge showed me pictures painted by his father.
usedThe gun used in the robbery has been found.
неправильные формыthe money stolen in the robbery · goods made in this factory · a house built in 1920

📦 there is / there was + такое придаточное

-ingThere were some children swimming in the river.
-ingIs there anybody waiting?
-edThere was a big red car parked outside the house.
left = осталосьWe've eaten nearly all the chocolates. There are only a few left.

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. Соедини два предложения в одно

1. A woman lives next door. She's a nurse. → The woman who lives next door is a nurse.
2. I bought a jacket. It doesn't fit me. → The jacket (that) I bought doesn't fit me.
3. We helped some people. Their car had broken down. → We helped some people whose car had broken down.
4. I grew up in a town. I went back there. → I went back to the town where I grew up.
5. My brother lives in Kyiv. He is a dentist. (брат один) → My brother, who lives in Kyiv, is a dentist.
6. She passed the exam. This surprised everyone. → She passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
7. He has three sisters. All of them are teachers. → He has three sisters, all of whom are teachers.
8. I was sitting next to a man. He talked all the time. → The man I was sitting next to talked all the time.

B. Можно ли выбросить who/that/which?

1. The book that I'm reading is great. → можно: The book I'm reading…
2. The bus that goes to the airport is number 5. → нельзя — это подлежащее
3. The people who we met were friendly. → можно: The people we met…
4. Tom, who I met yesterday, is from Ireland. → нельзя — это тип с запятыми
5. Everything that she said was true. → можно: Everything she said…
6. The man who lives upstairs plays the drums. → нельзя — подлежащее

C. Сократи до -ing / -ed clause

1. The man who is standing at the door → the man standing at the door
2. The letter which was sent yesterday → the letter sent yesterday
3. The road which connects the villages → the road connecting the villages
4. The money that was stolen in the robbery → the money stolen in the robbery
5. There is a car which is parked outside → there is a car parked outside

D. Исправь ошибку

1. Everything what he said was a lie. → Everything (that) he said…
2. The keys that you lost them are here. → The keys (that) you lost are here.
3. My father, that lives in Poland, is retired. → My father, who lives in Poland, …
4. I have a friend whose is a doctor. → I have a friend who is a doctor.
5. She didn't call, what was strange. → …, which was strange.
6. This is the house where I was born in. → … where I was born. / … that I was born in.
