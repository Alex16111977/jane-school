Passive — полный набор

Все формы пассива · I was given · I don't like being told · it is said that … · have something done

🔧

Пассив во всех временах

Формула одна: be в нужной форме + V3

Один принцип на весь пассив: берёшь ту форму глагола be, которая была бы у смыслового глагола в активе, и добавляешь V3 (past participle).
cleans → is cleaned · is cleaning → is being cleaned · has cleaned → has been cleaned · will clean → will be cleaned

🗂️ Таблица форм — глагол clean

ВремяActivePassive
Present simpleSomebody cleans the room.The room is cleaned every day.
Past simpleSomebody cleaned it.It was cleaned yesterday.
Present continuousSomebody is cleaning it.It is being cleaned now.
Past continuousSomebody was cleaning it.It was being cleaned when I arrived.
Present perfectSomebody has cleaned it.It has been cleaned.
Past perfectSomebody had cleaned it.It had been cleaned.
will / futureSomebody will clean it.It will be cleaned later.
going toThey are going to build it.It is going to be built.
МодальныйSomebody must do something.Something must be done.
Перфектный инфинитивSomebody should have cleaned it.It should have been cleaned.
-ing формаI hate people telling me…I hate being told…

🚫 Чего в английском НЕТ: пассива у Future continuous и Perfect continuous. It will be being cleaned, It has been being cleaned — так не говорят, перестраивают фразу.

💡 Пассив после модальных и в инфинитиве

must / should / canSomething must be done before it's too late.
can'tA mystery is something that can't be explained.
couldThe music could be heard from a long way away.
want toPlease go away. I want to be left alone.
might haveIt might have been sent to the wrong address.
wouldn't haveIf you had locked the car, it wouldn't have been stolen.
seem to haveThere were problems, but they seem to have been solved.

⚠️ Три классические ошибки

❌
  • The room is clean every day. (потеряли -ed)
  • My bike was stole. (V2 вместо V3)
  • I am born in Kyiv.
  • The problem is discussing now.
✅
  • The room is cleaned every day.
  • My bike was stolen.
  • I was born in Kyiv.
  • The problem is being discussed now.

🔑 I was born — всегда прошедшее, если речь про конкретного человека: Where were you born? Настоящее только про статистику: How many babies are born every day?

🎯

Зачем нужен пассив

И четыре конструкции, которые без него не собрать

📌 Когда выбирают пассив

исполнитель неизвестенA lot of money was stolen in the robbery. — кто украл, неизвестно
исполнитель неваженIs this room cleaned every day? — неважно, кто именно
важнее объектThis house was built in 1981. — речь о доме, не о строителе
процессы, наукаThe water is heated to 100°C and then filtered.
официальный тонYour application has been received. — никто не назван виноватым
тема остаётся та жеTom is very popular. He was invited to three parties last week.

💡 by добавляют, только если исполнитель реально важен: This house was built by my grandfather. В 80 % пассивных фраз by вообще нет.

🎁 Два дополнения: I was given …

Глаголы give, ask, offer, pay, show, tell, send, teach, promise, lend имеют два дополнения — значит, пассив можно построить двумя способами.

My grandfather gave me this watch. → I was given this watch.  /  This watch was given to me.
offerI've been offered the job, but I don't think I want it.
giveYou will be given plenty of time to decide.
showI didn't see the original, but I was shown a copy.
payHe's paid a lot of money to do very little.
tellWe weren't told about the change.
askI was asked a lot of difficult questions at the interview.

🔑 Чаще всего начинают с человека: I was given… звучит естественнее, чем This watch was given to me…

🔄 being done — пассивный -ing

activeI don't like people telling me what to do.
passiveI don't like being told what to do.
activeI remember somebody taking me to the zoo.
passiveI remember being taken to the zoo as a child.
hate being…Steve hates being kept waiting.
without being…We climbed over the wall without being seen.
after being…After being questioned for hours, he was released.
having been…Having been warned twice, he finally slowed down. формально

🆕 get вместо be — разговорный пассив

get + V3There was a fight, but nobody got hurt. = was hurt
get invitedI don't get invited to many parties.
get offeredI'm surprised Liz didn't get offered the job.
get stuck / caughtWe got caught in the rain and got stuck in traffic.

⚠️ get годится только там, где что-то происходит. Про состояния — нет:
Jessica gets liked by everybody → is liked.  Very little got known about him → was known.
📌 Отдельно живут выражения без пассивного смысла: get married, get divorced, get dressed, get changed, get lost, get ready.

📰

It is said that … / He is said to …

Язык новостей: «сообщается, что…», «как считается…»

Обе фразы значат одно и то же — People say that he is 108:

It is said that he is 108.  =  He is said to be 108.

Первый вариант начинается с пустого it, второй — с самого человека/предмета.

🗃️ Глаголы, которые так работают

saidbelievedthought knownexpectedreported allegedconsideredunderstood claimedestimatedrumoured
It … that …Подлежащее … to …
It is said that she runs 10 miles a day.She is said to run 10 miles a day.
It is believed that the boy is wearing a white sweater.The boy is believed to be wearing a white sweater.
It is expected that the strike will end soon.The strike is expected to end soon.
It is alleged that he stole a car.He is alleged to have stolen a car.
It is reported that two people were injured.Two people are reported to have been injured.
It is said that there is a secret tunnel.There is said to be a secret tunnel.

🔑 Правило перевода: если событие раньше момента речи → to have done; если одновременно → to do / to be doing; если позже → to do (обычно с will-глаголами: expected to end).

🎭 supposed to — два разных значения

1. = говорят, что…
  • I want to see that film. It's supposed to be good.
  • He's supposed to have robbed a bank years ago.
  • Fireworks are supposed to have been invented in China.
2. = так задумано / договорено
  • The plan is supposed to be a secret, but everyone knows.
  • What are you doing at work? You're supposed to be on holiday.
  • Our guests were supposed to come at 7.30, but they were late.
  • I'm supposed to be meeting Chris in ten minutes.

You're not supposed to… = так делать не положено / не советуют:
You're not supposed to park here. · He's better now, but he's not supposed to exercise too hard.

📝 Где это реально пригодится

эссе, осторожностьIt is widely believed that social media affects concentration.
пересказ слуховShe is rumoured to be leaving the company.
цифрыThe damage is estimated to be around €2 million.
новостиThe driver is reported to have been travelling at 120 km/h.
🏠

have something done

«Сделать что-то не своими руками» — конструкция, которой нет в русском

У Лизы протекала крыша. Она вызвала мастера, и он починил.

Lisa had the roof repaired.

= она организовала, чтобы починили. Сама не чинила.

самLisa repaired the roof.залезла и починила
через мастераLisa had the roof repaired.вызвала кого-то

🧩 Порядок слов — тут все ошибаются

have + объект + V3
❌
  • How often do you have serviced your car?
  • Our neighbour is having built a garage.
  • I have cut my hair every month. (= я сам себе стригу!)
✅
  • How often do you have your car serviced?
  • Our neighbour is having a garage built.
  • I have my hair cut every month.

Объект всегда между have и причастием. Проверка: если V3 стоит сразу после have — ты сказал что-то другое.

🕒 Во всех временах

presentWe have the house cleaned once a week.
continuousWe are having the house painted at the moment.
pastWhere did you have your hair cut?
present perfectWe've just had the car serviced.
futureI'll have the documents translated by Friday.
modalYou should have that coat cleaned.
-ingI don't like having my picture taken.

🔄 get something done — то же, но разговорнее

get + объект + V3When are you going to get the roof repaired?
советI think you should get your hair cut really short.
делаI need to get my eyes tested and get my phone fixed.

💡 Ещё одно значение get sth done — «доделать своими руками к сроку»: I must get this essay finished tonight.

💀 Второе значение: с тобой это случилось

Та же конструкция описывает неприятность, которую никто не заказывал:

кражаPaul and Karen had their bags stolen while travelling. — у них украли сумки
травмаGary had his nose broken in a fight.
вопросHave you ever had your bike stolen?
отказShe had her visa refused twice.

Как отличить: если действие в чью-то пользу и явно оплачено/заказано — значение «заказал». Если это ущерб — значение «с ним это произошло». Контекст решает всё.

🆓 Соседняя конструкция: have somebody do / get somebody to do

have sb doI'll have the porter carry your bags. — распоряжусь, чтобы носильщик отнёс (без to!)
get sb to doI'll get my brother to help us. — уговорю брата помочь
make sb doThey made me wait two hours. — заставили

Разница: have sb do — спокойное поручение (часто AmE), get sb to do — убедить/добиться, make sb do — заставить.

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. Активный → пассивный

1. Somebody has taken my umbrella. → My umbrella has been taken.
2. They are repairing the bridge. → The bridge is being repaired.
3. Nobody had cleaned the room. → The room hadn't been cleaned.
4. They will announce the results tomorrow. → The results will be announced tomorrow.
5. Someone should have told me. → I should have been told.
6. They gave Anna a prize. → Anna was given a prize. / A prize was given to Anna.
7. People say that he is a millionaire. → He is said to be a millionaire.
8. They think the thieves escaped through the window. → The thieves are thought to have escaped through the window.

B. have / get something done

1. Мне вчера подстригли волосы. → I had my hair cut yesterday.
2. Тебе стоит проверить глаза. → You should have / get your eyes tested.
3. Сейчас нам красят кухню. → We're having the kitchen painted at the moment.
4. Где ты чинил телефон? → Where did you have / get your phone fixed?
5. У него в Риме украли паспорт. → He had his passport stolen in Rome.
6. Я не люблю фотографироваться. → I don't like having my picture taken.

C. Исправь ошибку

1. The house is build in 1920. → The house was built in 1920.
2. I am born in 2001. → I was born in 2001.
3. The road is repairing at the moment. → The road is being repaired at the moment.
4. She got liked by everyone. → She was liked by everyone.
5. I had cut my hair last week. → I had my hair cut last week.
6. He is said that he is very rich. → He is said to be very rich. / It is said that he is very rich.
7. I hate to be told what to do. → I hate being told what to do.
8. The meeting will be took place on Monday. → The meeting will take place on Monday. (take place не бывает в пассиве)
