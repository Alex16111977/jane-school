Какой предлог после слова

listen TO · depend ON · good AT · reason FOR — полный справочник сочетаний с предлогами + случаи, где предлога нет

➡️

Глагол + to / at

И главный список: где предлога быть НЕ должно

🗣️ verb + TO

talk / speak to sbWho were you talking to? talk with тоже возможно
listen to sthI like to listen to the radio. не listen the radio
apologise to sb (for)They apologised to me for their mistake.
explain sth to sbCan you explain this word to me? не explain me this word
explain / describe to sb what…I explained to them why I was worried. · Let me describe to you what I saw.
write to sbI'll write to you when I get there.
reply to sthHe never replied to my email.
belong to sbThis bag belongs to Sarah.
happen to sb/sthWhat happened to that gold watch you had?
invite sb toThey only invited a few people to their wedding.
prefer A to BI prefer tea to coffee.

🚫 Здесь предлога НЕТ (а в русском/немецком есть)

❌ так говорят по ошибке
  • I called to the airline.
  • You didn't answer to my email.
  • Can I ask to you?
  • He thanked to me.
  • We discussed about the plan.
  • They entered into the room.
  • She married with an American.
✅ правильно
  • I called the airline. (phone / call / email / text sb)
  • You didn't answer my email.
  • Can I ask you?
  • He thanked me (for helping).
  • We discussed the plan.
  • They entered the room.
  • She married an American / is married to an American.

Запомни цепочку: phone / call / email / text / answer / ask / thank / discuss / enter / marry / reach / approach / lack — без предлога. Но: write to, reply to, listen to — с предлогом.

👁️ verb + AT

look / stare / glance atWhy are you looking at me like that?
have a look atCan you have a look at my essay?
laugh atEverybody will laugh at me with this haircut.
smile atShe smiled at me and walked on.
point / aim atDon't point that knife at me.
shoot / fire atHe was shooting at birds, but didn't hit any.

⭐ at или to — и смысл меняется

shout AT — агрессияHe got angry and started shouting at me.орал на меня
shout TO — чтобы услышалиHe shouted to me from the other side of the street.крикнул мне издалека
throw AT — чтобы попастьSomebody threw an egg at the politician.
throw TO — чтобы поймалиLisa threw the keys to me from the window.
💬

Глагол + about / for

И пары, где предлог полностью меняет смысл

💭 verb + ABOUT

talk / read / know aboutWe talked about a lot of things.
have a discussion aboutWe had a discussion about what to do. но: we discussed what to do
do something aboutIf it worries you, do something about it.
worry aboutDon't worry about me.
care aboutHe's selfish. He doesn't care about other people. = ему не важны

🔍 Три пары: hear / think / dream / complain / remind

hear ABOUT = узнать о событииDid you hear about the fire at the hotel?
hear OF = знать о существованииWho's Tom Hart? I've never heard of him.
hear FROM = получить весточкуHave you heard from Jane recently?
think ABOUT = обдумыватьI've thought about what you said.
think OF = придумать / оценитьIt was my idea — I thought of it first. · What did you think of the film?
dream ABOUT = видеть во снеI dreamt about you last night.
dream OF = мечтать / «ни за что»Do you dream of being rich? · I wouldn't dream of it!
complain ABOUT = жаловаться наWe complained about the food.
complain OF = жаловаться на больHe was complaining of a pain in his stomach.
remind sb ABOUT = напомнить не забытьThanks for reminding me about the meeting.
remind sb OF = напоминать (похож)This house reminds me of my childhood.

💡 think of / about doing — о планах: My sister is thinking of going to Canada. (оба варианта ок)

🎯 verb + FOR

ask (sb) forI emailed the company asking for more information. но: ask sb the way / the time
apply (to…) forWhy don't you apply for the job?
wait forI'm waiting for the rain to stop.
search … forI've searched the house for my keys.
leave (a place) forShe left home for work at seven. не left to work
pay forWe didn't have enough to pay for the meal. но: pay the bill / the rent / a fine
thank / forgive sb forI'll never forgive them for what they did.
apologise (to sb) forI apologised for my mistake.
blame sb forEverybody blamed me for the accident. = I was to blame for it.
blame sth on sbDon't blame it on me! внимание: проблему сваливают ON кого-то

👀 look for / look after / care for / take care of

look FOR = искатьI've lost my keys. Help me look for them.
look AFTER = присматриватьHe needs somebody to look after him.
take care OF = взять на себяI'll take care of the travel arrangements.
care FOR = ухаживать / не любить (в отриц.)She cares for her father. · I don't care for hot weather.

И отдельно: I don't care what/where/how… — без предлога: I don't care what you do.

🧩

Глагол + of / from / on / in / into / with

Оставшиеся семьи — и их надо просто узнавать

🔵 verb + OF

accuse sb ofTina accused me of being selfish.
suspect sb ofSome students were suspected of cheating.
approve / disapprove ofHis parents don't approve of what he does.
consist ofThe meal consisted of seven courses.
die of / from«What did he die of?» — «A heart attack.»

🟢 verb + FROM

suffer fromMore people are suffering from heart disease.
protect sb/sth fromSun cream protects the skin from the sun.
prevent sb fromThe rain prevented us from going out.
borrow sth from sbI borrowed the money from my brother. но: lend sb sth
translate from … intoHer books have been translated from Polish into many languages.

🟠 verb + ON

depend onIt depends on the traffic. но: It depends how much it is — on можно опустить перед how/what/where
rely onYou can rely on Anna — she keeps her promises.
live onHis salary isn't enough to live on.
congratulate sb onI congratulated her on her exam results.
compliment sb onI complimented Mark on his cooking.
concentrate onI tried to concentrate on my work.
insist onThey insisted on paying for the meal.
spend money onHow much do you spend on food each week? но: spend time doing

🟣 verb + IN / INTO

believe inDo you believe in ghosts? · I believe in saying what I think.
но: believe sth/sbThat can't be true — I don't believe it. не believe in it
specialise inShe specialises in company law.
succeed inI hope you succeed in finding the job you want.
break intoOur house was broken into last week.
crash / drive / run intoHe lost control and crashed into a wall.
divide / cut / split intoThe book is divided into three parts.

🟡 verb + WITH

collide withA bus collided with a car.
fill sth withFill the pan with water. но прилагательное: full of water
provide / supply sb withThe school provides students with books.
agree with sbI agree with you about that. но: agree to a plan, agree on a date
deal withWho's going to deal with this complaint?
🔤

Прилагательное + предлог

good AT · married TO · afraid OF · keen ON

⭐ nice OF you — и nice TO me

OF somebody TO do — оценка поступкаIt was very nice of you to help me.nice / kind / good / generous / polite / stupid / silly of sb
TO somebody — отношение к человекуThey have always been very nice to me.не nice with me

Ещё: It was stupid of me to go out without a coat. · Why were you so unfriendly to Lucy?

😡 Чувства: about / with

angry / annoyed about sthThere's no point getting angry about things that don't matter.
angry / annoyed with sb forAre you annoyed with me for being late?
upset aboutLisa is upset about not being invited.
excited / worried / nervous aboutAre you nervous about the exam?
pleased / happy / delighted withThey were delighted with the present.
disappointed / satisfied withWere you happy with your exam results?
surprised / shocked at / byEverybody was surprised at (or by) the news.
impressed with / byI'm very impressed with her English.
fed up / bored withI'm fed up with this job.
tired ofLet's go — I'm tired of waiting.

😔 sorry — четыре схемы

sorry about (ситуация)I'm sorry about the mess. · Sorry about the delay.
sorry for/about (мой поступок)I'm sorry for shouting at you.
sorry + предложениеI'm sorry I shouted at you.
feel sorry for sbI feel sorry for Mark — he's had a lot of bad luck.
sorry to hearI'm sorry to hear about your job.

🔵 adjective + OF

afraid / scared / frightened / terrified of fond ofproud ofashamed of jealous / envious ofsuspicious ofcritical of tolerant ofaware / conscious ofcapable / incapable of full ofshort oftypical ofsure / certain of / about
terrified of«Are you afraid of spiders?» — «Yes, I'm terrified of them.»
aware ofI wasn't aware of that.
capable ofYou are capable of doing the job well.
full of / short ofShe's always full of energy. · I'm a bit short of money.
typical ofIt's typical of him to keep everybody waiting.

🌈 Остальные предлоги

good / bad / brilliant ATI'm not very good at repairing things. не good in
married / engaged TOLouise is married to an American. но: married with three children = у неё трое детей
similar TOYour handwriting is similar to mine.
different FROM / TOThe film was different from what I'd expected. (AmE: different than)
interested INAre you interested in art?
keen ONChris wasn't keen on going out.
dependent ON / independent OFI don't want to be dependent on anybody.
crowded WITHThe streets were crowded with tourists. но: full of tourists
famous FORFlorence is famous for its art treasures.
responsible FORWho was responsible for all that noise?
📦

Существительное + предлог

reason FOR · cause OF · increase IN · solution TO

📋 По предлогам

ПредлогСуществительныеПример
FORa reason, a need, a demand, an excuseNobody knew the reason for the delay. (не reason of)
OFthe cause, a picture/photo/map/plan, an advantageThe cause of the explosion is unknown. · a photo of her family
INan increase, a decrease, a rise, a fallThere's been an increase in road accidents.
TOdamage, an invitation, a solution, a key, an answer, a reply, a reaction, an attitudeI hope we find a solution to the problem. (не solution of)
WITHa relationship, a connection, contactDo you have a good relationship with your parents?
BETWEENa difference, a connection, a relationship (двух вещей)There are differences between British and American English.

⚠️ Самые частые ошибки

❌
  • the reason of the delay
  • a solution of the problem
  • an answer of the question
  • an increase of accidents
  • the damage of the car
✅
  • the reason for the delay
  • a solution to the problem
  • an answer to the question
  • an increase in accidents
  • the damage to the car

💡 Тонкость: the advantage of living alone (преимущество чего-то), но there are advantages in / to living alone (у этого есть плюсы).
💡 attitude to = attitude towards — оба варианта верны.

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. Вставь предлог (или поставь «—», если он не нужен)

1. I'll phone _____ you tomorrow. → — (phone you)
2. Please listen _____ me. → to
3. Can you explain this rule _____ me? → to
4. We discussed _____ the problem for an hour. → —
5. She apologised _____ me _____ being late. → to … for
6. He's married _____ a doctor. → to
7. I'm not very good _____ maths. → at
8. It depends _____ the weather. → on
9. They accused him _____ lying. → of
10. Who's responsible _____ this mess? → for
11. The bag was full _____ books. → of
12. The room was crowded _____ people. → with
13. I didn't answer _____ his letter. → — (answer his letter)
14. She replied _____ my message an hour later. → to
15. What's the reason _____ the delay? → for
16. There's been a rise _____ prices. → in

B. Выбери правильную пару

1. Have you _____ (heard about / heard of / heard from) Jane? She called me yesterday. → heard from
2. Never _____ him — who is he? → heard of
3. This song _____ me _____ my childhood. → reminds … of
4. Can you _____ me _____ the meeting tomorrow? → remind … about
5. He shouted _____ me because he was furious. → at
6. She shouted _____ me from across the street. → to
7. I'm _____ my old laptop — time for a new one. (надоел) → fed up with / tired of
8. Don't _____ it _____ me — it wasn't my fault. → blame … on
