Порядок слов

Глагол и дополнение не разлучают · место перед временем · always / also / probably — в середину

🔗

Глагол и дополнение — неразлучны

Между ними ничего не вставляют

Правило: в английском verb + object идут вплотную. Всё остальное (как, где, когда) — после дополнения.

❌
  • I like very much my job.
  • Our guide spoke fluently English.
  • I lost also my passport.
  • You'll see on your left a supermarket.
  • He speaks very well English.
✅
  • I like my job very much.
  • Our guide spoke English fluently.
  • I also lost my passport.
  • You'll see a supermarket on your left.
  • He speaks English very well.

Русский порядок слов свободный, английский — нет. Это самая частая «иностранная» ошибка в речи.

📍 Базовая цепочка английского предложения

кто → глагол → дополнение → как → где → когда
КтоГлаголЧтоКакГдеКогда
Shereadthe lettercarefullyin the kitchenlast night.
Wetookthe children—to the zooon Sunday.
Ididn't usemy phone——yesterday.
Do youeatmeat——every day?

🔑 Полная формула для запоминания: SVOMPT — Subject, Verb, Object, Manner, Place, Time.

🧩 Два дополнения

кому + чтоShe gave me the keys. без предлога: сначала человек
что + to комуShe gave the keys to me. с предлогом: сначала вещь
местоимение-вещьShe gave it to me. не gave me it в стандартном варианте
так жеsend / show / tell / lend / offer / buy sb sth = … sth to/for sb
verb + objectI read the article twice before I understood it.
verb + objectShe speaks Italian almost perfectly.
verb + objectWe booked the tickets online last night.
verb + object + mannerHe closed the door quietly behind him.
🗺️

Сначала место, потом время

Place before time — в английском наоборот, чем в русском

WHERE  →  WHEN
✅Ben walks to work every morning.
❌Ben walks every morning to work.
✅I'm going to Paris on Monday.
❌I'm going on Monday to Paris.
✅They've lived in the same house for years.
✅We need to be at the airport by 8.

🔄 Время можно вынести в начало

в начале — акцентOn Monday I'm going to Paris.
в началеAfter the party Sarah gave me a lift home.
в конце — нейтральноSarah gave me a lift home after the party.
place + timeThey moved to Berlin last spring.
place + timeI work from home on Fridays.
place + timeWe arrived at the hotel just before midnight.
time в началеEvery Sunday we have lunch at my parents' house.

⚠️ А вот место в начало выносят редко — только в описаниях: In the corner stood an old piano.

📌 Глагол + место идут вместе

verb + placego home · live in a city · walk to work · arrive at the station
verb + object + placeWe took the children to the zoo. не took to the zoo the children
verb + object + placeDon't put anything on the table. · Did you learn English at school?

📏 Внутри времени: от малого к большому

время → день → датаThe train leaves at 6.30 on Monday morning.
как долго → как часто → когдаI worked for two hours every evening last week.

Внутри места — наоборот, от точного к общему: She lives in a small flat in the centre of Berlin.

Наречия в середине предложения

always · also · probably · never · already · even

Часть наречий не может стоять ни в начале, ни в конце — их место рядом с глаголом: always, usually, often, never, rarely, already, still, just, also, probably, definitely, almost, even, all, both.

1️⃣ Один глагол → наречие ПЕРЕД ним

alwaysEmily always drives to work.
almostI almost fell going down the stairs.
alsoI cleaned the house and also cooked dinner. не cooked also
hardly ever / rarelyLaura hardly ever watches TV and rarely reads newspapers.
already«Shall I give you my address?» — «No, I already have it.»
перед have toJoe never phones me. I always have to phone him. не I have always to

2️⃣ am / is / are / was / were → наречие ПОСЛЕ

alsoWe were tired and we were also hungry. не also were
always / neverYou're always late. You're never on time.
usuallyThe traffic isn't usually this bad.

3️⃣ Два и больше глаголов → после ПЕРВОГО

can neverI can never remember her name.
после первого глаголаShe has always wanted to live by the sea.
после первого глаголаI would never have guessed his age.
после первого глаголаWe are still waiting for an answer.
после первого глаголаYou should definitely try the soup.
doesn't usuallyClare doesn't usually drive to work.
are definitelyAre you definitely going away next week?
will probablyThe meeting will probably be cancelled.
have alwaysYou've always been very kind to me.
can't evenHe can't even boil an egg.
do stillDo you still work for the same company?
is alreadyThe house was only built a year ago and it's already falling down.

⚠️ probably и отрицание

✅I probably won't see you.
✅I'll probably not see you.
❌I won't probably see you.

Правило: probably становится перед отрицательной формой (isn't / won't / doesn't).

👥 all и both — туда же, в середину

перед смысловым глаголомWe all felt ill after the meal. не felt all ill
после beMy parents are both teachers. · My friends are all going out tonight.
после первого глаголаSarah and Jane have both applied for the job.

🔁 Когда глагол заменён на is / will / did

always перед нимHe says he won't be late, but he always is.
never перед нимI've never done it and I never will.
сравниTom says he isn't clever, but I think he is.

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. Собери предложение правильно

1. (very much / I / this song / like) → I like this song very much.
2. (to school / every day / walks / she) → She walks to school every day.
3. (English / fluently / speaks / he) → He speaks English fluently.
4. (at the airport / we / at six / arrived) → We arrived at the airport at six.
5. (my keys / on the table / I / left) → I left my keys on the table.
6. (to the cinema / took / on Sunday / the kids / they) → They took the kids to the cinema on Sunday.

B. Поставь слово в скобках на правильное место

1. (always) She is late for meetings. → She is always late…
2. (never) I have seen such a mess. → I have never seen…
3. (also) I speak Polish. → I also speak Polish.
4. (probably) He won't come tonight. → He probably won't come tonight.
5. (usually) Do you get up early? → Do you usually get up early?
6. (both) They are studying medicine. → They are both studying medicine.
7. (all) We were surprised. → We were all surprised.
8. (even) He didn't say hello. → He didn't even say hello.
9. (always) I have to remind him. → I always have to remind him.
10. (already) The film had started. → The film had already started.

C. Найди ошибку

1. I like very much this city. → I like this city very much.
2. She speaks well English. → She speaks English well.
3. We go every summer to Italy. → We go to Italy every summer.
4. He always is complaining. → He is always complaining.
5. I have always to wait for him. → I always have to wait for him.
6. I won't probably be there. → I probably won't be there.
7. They felt all tired. → They all felt tired.
8. I bought yesterday a new laptop. → I bought a new laptop yesterday.
