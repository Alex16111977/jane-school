Clauses

Как соединять два предложения: время, причина, цель + конструкции с that. Основа для длинных фраз в эссе.

⏰

Придаточные времени

when · while · as · as soon as · until · by the time

Придаточное = часть предложения с собственным подлежащим и глаголом.
We could play cards afterwards. (наречие)
We could play cards after the meal. (фраза)
We could play cards after we've eaten. (придаточное)

🔗 Все союзы времени

when
Завершённое действие или «каждый раз».
When I arrived, the party was in full swing. · When you dial the number, no one answers.It hurts when I laugh.
while / as
Длительное действие, на фоне которого что-то происходит.
The doorbell rang as/while I was changing. · While Ann was in hospital, she had a visit from her teacher.
as
«По мере того как» — одно меняется вместе с другим.
As we drove further north, the weather got worse.
just as
«Ровно в тот момент».
Just as we came out of the theatre, the rain started.
whenever /
every time
«Каждый раз» — эмфатичнее, чем when.
Whenever/Every time Max calls, he brings me flowers.
as soon as /
immediately
«Как только».
I'll come and see you as soon as I've finished work. · As soon as/Immediately the gates were open, the crowds rushed in.
the minute /
the moment
The minute/The moment you hear any news, let me know.
before / after
Have some coffee before you go. · It always rains after I've washed my car.
by the time
«К тому моменту как».
I've usually left the house by the time the postman comes.НЕ by the postman comes
once
Once you've learnt to swim, you'll never forget.
since
Lots has happened since I last saw you.
till / until
Till/Until the cheque arrives, I can't pay my rent.

📌 before, after, since, till, until бывают и предлогами: Lots has happened since your last visit.
📌 После before, after, since можно герундий: I always have a shower after taking exercise.

⚠️ Будущее после этих союзов НЕ ставится

НЕТДА
as soon as you will have newsCall me as soon as you have any news.
until the rain will have stoppedLet's wait until the rain has stopped.
when he will retireHe'll move to the seaside when he retires.

🔗 Подробнее об этом — на странице The Future.

✂️ Сокращённые придаточные

С союзами when, while, once, until, where, if, although подлежащее и be можно выбросить.

Take care when crossing the road.= when you cross
Please wait until told to proceed.= until you are told
A car must be taxed when (it is) on the road.—
While waiting, Colin paced up and down.—
Check it again to make sure.инфинитив вместо придаточного
Having glanced at the letter, Helen pushed it aside.совсем без союза

🔗 Про причастные обороты подробно — на странице Participles.

⚡ no sooner / hardly — «едва… как»

Martin had no sooner sat down than the phone rang.обычный порядок
No sooner had Martin sat down than the phone rang.с инверсией — литературно
I had hardly started work when I felt a pain in my back.—
Hardly had I started work when I felt a pain.с инверсией

⚠️ После no sooner идёт than, после hardly — when. Первое действие в past perfect.

📍 Где ставить придаточное

В начале — с запятойIf you like, we could play cards.
В конце — без запятойWe could play cards if you like.

🔑 Выбор зависит от того, что новое. Новое ставим в конец:
I arrived ten minutes late. I was late because Don was telling me his problems. (новость — про Дона)
You know how Don talks. Well, because he was telling me his problems, I was late. (новость — что я опоздала)

🎯

Причина, цель и результат

because · so that · in order to — и чем они отличаются

Три разные вещи, которые легко перепутать:
ПРИЧИНА I made mistakes because I was tired. (почему)
ЦЕЛЬ He wore dark glasses so that no one would recognize him. (зачем)
РЕЗУЛЬТАТ I was tired, so I made mistakes. (что вышло)

❓ Причина

because
Самый обычный союз. Только им отвечают на вопрос why.
I made mistakes because I was tired.Why did you make so many mistakes? ~ (Because) I was tired.
since / as
Причина уже известна собеседнику — звучит мягче.
Since no one else came, I didn't tell them. · As the weather is often warm, many homes have swimming pools.
seeing (that) /
now (that)
Seeing (that) it's so late, why don't you stay the night?
Now (that) I've finished the course, I have to look for a job.
for
Формально и старомодно; всегда после главного предложения.
The soldiers were exhausted, for they had marched a long way.
предлоги
because of / due to / owing to / on account of / in view of + существительное
The scheme was abandoned because of the cost. · due to the fact that it was unpopular
причастие
Being tired, I made mistakes. · Having finished the course, I have to look for a job.

⚠️ Позиция because может менять смысл:
I didn't go to the exhibition because I was busy. (не пошла, потому что была занята)
I didn't go to the exhibition because I was interested. I went there to see Sandra. (пошла, но не из-за интереса)
📌 considering работает и как союз, и как предлог, и как наречие:
Considering (that) he's seventy, George is remarkably fit. · Considering his age… · He's remarkably fit, considering.

🎯 Цель — «зачем»

to + инфинитив — самый простой
I'd just sat down to read the paper.
in order to / so as to — чуть формальнее
The company borrowed money in order to finance their advertising.
Paul wore a suit so as to make a good impression.
(In order) to save time we'll fax all the information.
отрицание — только in order not to / so as not to
I wrote it in my diary so as not to forget.
НЕЛЬЗЯ I wrote it not to forget.
so that + подлежащее — когда деятели разные
You should keep milk in a fridge so that it stays fresh.
Why don't you take a day off so that you can recover properly?
Moira left some salad so that James could eat it later.
for + существительное или герундий
We went out for some fresh air. · Why not come over for a chat?
A saw is a tool for cutting wood. (назначение вещи вообще)
I need a saw to cut this wood. (конкретная задача — инфинитив!)

⚠️ После so that идёт полное предложение, часто с will, would, can, could.
🔑 Разница for + герундий и to + инфинитив:
ДА The small scale is for weighing letters. (для чего эта штука вообще)
ДА I got the scale out to weigh the letter. (зачем я её достала сейчас)
НЕТ I got the scale out for weighing the letter.
📌 Вместо so that… not часто говорят to avoid: He kept his shirt on to avoid getting sunburnt.

⚖️ Цель или результат?

Цель — so thatI took a day off so that I could recover properly.я специально взял выходной
Результат — soThe car simply refused to start, so I couldn't get to work.так вышло, я этого не хотел

Правило простое: so that = цель, so = результат.
Ещё способ выразить результат: The talk was so boring that I fell asleep. · It was such a boring talk that I fell asleep.

🌍

Место, образ действия и «неважно кто»

where · as if · whoever · no matter what

📍 Место

Where the road bends left, there's a turning on the right.—
Sue parked the car where she had the day before.—
Sebastian takes the teddy bear everywhere he goes.—

🎭 Образ действия

Do it (in) the way (that) I showed you.—
Why can't I live my life how I want to live it?—
Jessica behaved as/like she always does.like — разговорно
How can you act as if/as though nothing had happened?—
You look as if/like you've seen a ghost.—
It looks like it's going to be a nice day.= It looks like being a nice day.

⚠️ В британском формальном письме like как союз избегают — надёжнее as:
It is safer to use as. There was trouble at the carnival, as there was last year.
Но как предлог like нормально: Like last year, there was trouble.

💬 Комментарий и оговорки

As you know, things are difficult just now.—
Putting it another way, why should I bother?—
To tell you the truth, I don't think there's much chance.—
As far as I can tell, there's nothing wrong.—
The party was a disappointment in that / in so far as the celebrity guest didn't turn up.= в том смысле, что
The car's all right, except (that) the heater doesn't work.—

🔄 whoever, whatever — «неважно кто/что»

whoever whatever whichever whenever wherever however
Whoever plays in goal, we're bound to lose.кто бы ни играл
I won't change my mind whatever you say.что бы ты ни говорил
Whenever I ring Tracy, she's never there.когда бы я ни звонил
I can't draw faces, however hard I try.как бы я ни старался
Whoever suggested the idea, it's still nonsense.—

То же самое можно сказать через no matter:

I won't change my mind no matter what you say.= whatever you say
No matter where we go on holiday, you never like it.= wherever we go

🔗 Условие и уступка — кратко

Условиеif, unless, as long as, provided (that), in case, on condition that
You can picnic here as long as you don't leave litter.
Уступкаalthough, though, even though, whereas, while, in spite of the fact that
Although shocked by the news, I was greatly relieved.
Инверсия вместо ifShould it rain, the reception will be held indoors.
Had you signed, you'd have lost your rights. · Were I you, I'd say nothing.

📦

Придаточные с that и wh-

Целое предложение вместо существительного

Идея: там, где могло бы стоять существительное, ставим целое предложение.
We didn't know the time. (существительное)
We didn't know what time it was. (придаточное)
⚠️ Порядок слов внутри — как в утверждении: НЕ what time was it

🧱 Три вида

Начинается сСоответствуетПример
thatутверждениюI expected that we would be late.
wh-словоwh-вопросуWe didn't know what time it was.
if / whetheryes/no-вопросуWe'll have to decide if/whether we can afford it.

📌 В неформальном английском that часто выбрасывают: I knew (that) you wouldn't like this colour.

📋 Семь схем

1. Дополнение глагола — самое частое
I noticed that the door was open. · No one believes (that) the project will go ahead.
Tim wouldn't say where he was going. · I wonder whether that's a good idea.
2. После be — именная часть
The idea is that we take it in turns. · The truth is (that) I don't get on with my flat-mate.
The difficulty was how Emma was going to find us.
3. Подлежащее — звучит тяжеловесно
That he could be mistaken didn't seem possible.
That everyone got back safely was a great relief.
Здесь that убирать НЕЛЬЗЯ.
4. С пустым it — так говорят обычно
It didn't seem possible that he could be mistaken.
It was a great relief that everyone got back safely.
It's hard to say whether it's going to rain. · It's nice (that) you've got some time off.
5. После предлога — только wh- или whether
We had a discussion about who should be invited.
The government is looking into what needs to be done.
He made no comment on whether a decision had been reached.
НЕЛЬЗЯ about if   about that Nicola was ill → No one told me about Nicola's illness / (that) Nicola was ill.
6. После прилагательного
I was ashamed that I'd let my friends down. · I'm glad (that) you enjoyed the meal.
We were worried (that) there were no life guards. · Lucy was sure (that) she could identify him.
7. После существительного
You can't deny the fact that you received the message.
The news that the plane had crashed came as a terrible shock.
I heard a rumour that there's been a leak of radioactivity.
Здесь that тоже обычно не убирают.

😊 Прилагательные, после которых идёт that

afraid amused annoyed anxious aware certain confident conscious convinced delighted determined eager glad happy horrified impatient pleased proud sorry sure surprised willing

После sure и certain можно и wh-слово: I wasn't sure when the visitors would arrive.
А после некоторых — восклицательное how/what: I was surprised how upset Tom seemed. · Melissa was aware what a difficult task she faced.

⚠️ Отрицание переезжает в главное предложение

Так говорятI don't think we've got time.отрицание в главном
Так почти не говорятI think we haven't got time.грамматически верно, но звучит странно

Так работают think и believe.
С suppose, imagine, expect можно и так, и так: I don't suppose you're used to this weather. / I suppose you aren't used to this weather.

📝 Глаголы, после которых идёт that-clause

acceptadmitagreeannounce argueassumebelieveclaim complainconfirmdecidedeclare demanddiscoverdoubtexpect explainfearforgetguarantee guesshopeimplyinsist knowlearnmeanmention noticepoint outpredictpretend promiseproverealizerecognize recommendregretrememberreveal saysuggestsupposesuspect swearthreatenunderstandwarn

⚠️ После некоторых глаголов that-clause невозможен — там только инфинитив или герундий: aim, avoid, finish, involve, offer, refuse.
📌 С explain, complain, mention, point out, suggest человек упоминается через to:
We explained to the driver that we hadn't any money.
А с tell, remind, assure, convince, inform, persuade, promise, warn — напрямую:
We told the driver that we hadn't any money.
