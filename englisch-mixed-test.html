Времена и формы глагола

Ответ спрятан — сначала скажи вслух, потом кликни

Как работать со страницей: это сводная тренировка по всем темам сразу — так же, как в разделе Additional exercises в учебниках. Не подглядывай сразу: сначала произнеси ответ вслух, потом открывай.

A. Поставь глагол в нужное время

1. Listen! Somebody is knocking (knock) at the door.
2. Water boils (boil) at 100 degrees.
3. I was watching (watch) TV when the lights went out.
4. She has lived (live) in Vienna since 2019.
5. How long have you been waiting (you / wait)? — About twenty minutes.
6. When I got to the station, the train had already left (already / leave).
7. This time next week I will be lying (lie) on the beach.
8. By the end of the month they will have finished (finish) the project.
9. I 'm seeing (see) the dentist tomorrow at 10. — договорённость
10. Look at those clouds! It 's going to rain (rain).
11. I'll call you when I get (get) home. — после when нет will
12. He has been working (work) here for ten years and still loves it.

B. Present perfect или past simple?

1. I lost (lose) my keys yesterday.
2. I 've lost (lose) my keys — I can't get in.
3. Mozart wrote (write) more than 600 pieces of music.
4. My brother is a writer. He has written (write) five novels so far.
5. Did you see (you / see) Anna at the party last night?
6. Have you ever been (you / ever / be) to Japan?

C. used to / be used to / get used to

1. I used to smoke, but I gave up five years ago.
2. It was hard at first, but now I 'm used to working nights.
3. Don't worry, you'll soon get used to driving on the left.
4. There used to be a bakery here.

Модальные · условные · пассив

Три самые «экзаменационные» темы

A. Модальные глаголы

1. You've been travelling all day — you must be exhausted.
2. She's not answering. She might be in a meeting.
3. That can't be Tom — he's in Spain.
4. You should have told me earlier! — упрёк о прошлом
5. We needn't have hurried — the train was late anyway.
6. I 'd better leave now, or I'll miss the bus.
7. When I was a child, I could swim very well.
8. He must have forgotten about the meeting — he never showed up.

B. Условные и wish

1. If it rains tomorrow, we'll stay in.
2. If I had more money, I'd travel more.
3. If she had studied harder, she would have passed.
4. I wish I knew the answer.
5. I wish you hadn't said that yesterday.
6. I wish the neighbours would turn the music down!
7. I'd rather you didn't smoke in here.
8. It's time we went home.
9. If I'd caught that flight, I would be in Rome now. — mixed

C. Пассив

1. My bike was stolen (steal) last week.
2. The road is being repaired (repair) at the moment.
3. The parcel hasn't been delivered (not / deliver) yet.
4. Something must be done (must / do) about this.
5. He is said to be the richest man in the country.
6. I had my hair cut (have / hair / cut) yesterday.
7. They had their car stolen while they were on holiday.
8. I don't like being interrupted (interrupt) when I'm working.

-ing / to · артикли · relative clauses

Структуры, из которых собирается длинная фраза

A. -ing или to …?

1. I really enjoy cooking (cook).
2. We've decided not to move (not / move) house.
3. Would you mind waiting (wait) a moment?
4. Remember to lock (lock) the door when you leave.
5. I clearly remember meeting (meet) her at a conference.
6. My laptop needs repairing (repair).
7. It's no use worrying (worry) about it.
8. I'm looking forward to hearing (hear) from you.
9. She let me use (use) her phone.
10. They made us wait (wait) outside — but: we were made to wait outside.
11. He apologised for being (be) late.
12. I'd rather stay (stay) at home tonight.

B. Артикль: a / an, the или ничего?

1. She's an architect.
2. — Life is short.
3. Can you turn off the light, please?
4. We went to — bed early. — go to bed без артикля
5. He plays the piano beautifully.
6. I go to work by — car.
7. The Netherlands are famous for tulips.
8. What a beautiful day!

C. Relative clauses

1. The woman who / that lives upstairs is a vet.
2. Everything that he told us was true. — не what
3. My laptop, which I bought last year, has stopped working.
4. That's the man whose son plays in our team.
5. She was late again, which annoyed everybody.
6. The people (who/that) we met on holiday sent us a postcard. — можно выбросить
7. The man standing (stand) by the window is my boss.
8. Most of the goods made (make) here are exported.

D. Количество и определители

1. There isn't much milk left.
2. Few people came — the room was almost empty.
3. I've got a few ideas — want to hear them?
4. Neither of the two answers was correct.
5. Every student in the class passed.
6. Would you like some coffee? — предложение

Предлоги и фразовые глаголы

То, что нельзя вывести логикой — только натренировать

A. Вставь предлог (или «—»)

1. She's very good at chess.
2. It depends on the weather.
3. Are you interested in photography?
4. I apologised to her for being late.
5. Who's responsible for this mess?
6. Let me pay for dinner.
7. We discussed — the problem for an hour.
8. He married — a colleague — he's married to a colleague.
9. They arrived in Berlin on Tuesday and at the hotel at midnight.
10. What's the reason for the delay?
11. There's been an increase in prices.
12. We finally found a solution to the problem.
13. See you — next Monday.
14. The meeting is on 5 May at 9 o'clock.
15. I heard it on the radio.
16. He did it on purpose, not by accident.

B. Фразовый глагол вместо обычного

1. The car stopped working on the motorway. → broke down
2. They cancelled the concert. → called it off
3. Don't delay it any longer. → put it off
4. I need to find this word in the dictionary. → look it up
5. She invented the whole story. → made it up
6. He didn't arrive. → turn up / show up
7. We have to tolerate the noise. → put up with it
8. They rejected my offer. → turned it down
9. I was raised by my grandmother. → brought up
10. We've used all the milk. → run out of milk

C. Порядок слов — собери предложение

1. (I / this film / very much / like) → I like this film very much.
2. (she / to work / every day / cycles) → She cycles to work every day.
3. (always / he / is / complaining) → He is always complaining.
4. (probably / I / won't / be / late) → I probably won't be late.
5. (we / all / were / tired) → We were all tired.

Найди ошибку

20 предложений — в каждом ровно одна ошибка

1. I am living here since 2020. → I have lived / have been living here since 2020.
2. She said me that she was tired. → She told me / said to me…
3. If I would have time, I'd help you. → If I had time…
4. I wish I would know the answer. → I wish I knew the answer.
5. The room is clean every day. → The room is cleaned every day.
6. I'm looking forward to see you. → … to seeing you.
7. He suggested me to take a taxi. → He suggested taking a taxi.
8. I'm used to live alone. → I'm used to living alone.
9. My parents didn't let me to go out. → … didn't let me go out.
10. Everything what she said was true. → Everything (that) she said…
11. Despite of the rain, we went out. → Despite the rain / In spite of the rain…
12. Although the traffic, we arrived on time. → Despite the traffic / Although the traffic was bad…
13. I'll call you when I will arrive. → … when I arrive.
14. It was so a good film. → It was such a good film. / It was so good.
15. He's not enough old to drive. → He's not old enough to drive.
16. The soup was too hot to eat it. → … too hot to eat.
17. We arrived to the airport at six. → We arrived at the airport…
18. What time did you get to home? → … get home?
19. It was a lot of people there. → There were a lot of people there.
20. He is a friend of me. → He is a friend of mine.
