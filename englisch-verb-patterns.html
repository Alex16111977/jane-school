-ing или to …

enjoy doing · decide to do · remember doing/to do · used to vs be used to · prefer / would rather · предлог + -ing

🔁

Verb + -ing

enjoy doing · avoid doing · keep doing

Логика: эти глаголы говорят о самом занятии — о процессе, который уже существует или мыслится как факт.
I enjoy reading. (не enjoy to read) · Chris suggested going to the cinema. (не suggested to go)

📋 Список: только -ing

Часто в речи
  • enjoy — I enjoy cooking.
  • mind — Would you mind closing the door?
  • suggest — She suggested taking a taxi.
  • stop — Suddenly everybody stopped talking.
  • finish — I've finished cleaning the flat.
  • avoid — He avoided answering my question.
  • fancy — I don't fancy going out tonight.
  • keep (on) — You keep interrupting me.
  • practise — She practises speaking every day.
  • miss — I miss living by the sea.
Формальнее / письменно
  • consider — Have you considered moving abroad?
  • admit — He admitted taking the money.
  • deny — They denied doing anything wrong.
  • recommend — I recommend booking early.
  • imagine — I can't imagine living there.
  • risk — Don't risk losing your job.
  • postpone / put off — They put off making a decision.
  • involve — The job involves travelling a lot.
  • resent — She resents being told what to do.
  • can't stand / can't bear — I can't stand waiting.

💡 Фразовые глаголы почти всегда тянут -ing: give up smoking, put off telling him, go on / carry on working, keep on trying, end up paying twice.

🚫 Отрицание: not + -ing

not + -ingOn holiday I enjoy not having to get up early.
not + -ingHe apologised for not telling the truth.
не такI enjoy don't having… / I don't enjoy having… — это уже другой смысл

👥 verb + somebody + -ing

stop sb doingYou can't stop people doing what they want.
imagine sb doingI can't imagine George riding a motorbike.
remember sb doingI don't remember her saying that.
keep sb doingSorry to keep you waiting so long.
enjoyI enjoy walking to work when the weather is nice.
avoidHe avoids driving in the city centre.
don't mindI don't mind waiting another ten minutes.
give upShe gave up trying to explain.
can't standI can't stand queueing at the post office.

⏪ having done — когда действие явно раньше

= admitted stealingThey admitted having stolen the money.
= regret sayingI regret having said that.

Форма с having необязательна: обычная -ing форма прекрасно передаёт прошлое, если контекст ясен.

🔀 Альтернатива через that

У admit, deny, suggest, recommend есть вторая схема — с придаточным:

denyThey denied (that) they had done anything wrong.
suggestChris suggested (that) we go to the cinema.
recommendI recommend (that) you travel by train.

⚠️ Но никогда: She suggested me to go. Только suggested going или suggested that I go.

➡️

Verb + to …

decide to · manage to · want somebody to

Логика: to … смотрит вперёд — намерение, план, попытка, обещание, отказ. Действие ещё не факт.

📋 Список: только to …

План и решение
  • decide to take a taxi
  • plan to move next year
  • arrange to meet at six
  • hope to see you soon
  • intend to finish today
  • aim to improve my English
Реакция и результат
  • agree to help him
  • refuse to answer
  • offer to drive me home
  • promise not to be late
  • threaten to call the police
  • manage to open the door
  • fail to attract her attention
  • afford to buy a car
  • deserve to win
  • learn (how) to drive
  • forget to lock the door
  • tend to talk too much

Отрицание ставится перед to: We decided not to go out. · I promised not to be late.

🎭 seem / appear / pretend / claim

простой инфинитивThey seem to have plenty of money.
длительныйI pretended to be reading the newspaper.
перфектныйI seem to have lost my keys. = кажется, я их потерял
decideWe decided to stay one more night.
manageShe managed to finish the report before lunch.
refuseHe refused to answer any questions.
can't affordI can't afford to take a taxi every day.
promiseThey promised not to be late again.
отрицаниеShe claimed not to have seen me. · Ann pretended not to see me.

❓ Вопросительное слово + to …

how toWe asked how to get to the station.
where toHave you decided where to go on holiday?
whether toI don't know whether to apply or not.
what toDo you understand what to do?
show/tell sb how toCan somebody show me how to use this camera?

Работают так: ask, know, decide, remember, forget, learn, explain, understand, wonder + show / tell / ask / advise / teach somebody.

👤 verb + object + to …

I want you to help me.  —  не I want that you help me
want / would likeDo you want me to come with you? · Would you like me to go now?
expectWe expected Dan to be late.
askI asked him to wait outside.
tell / remind / warnCan you remind me to call Sam? · He warned me not to touch it.
adviseI wouldn't advise you to stay there.
invite / encourage / persuadeThey invited us to join them.
force / orderNobody forced you to sign it.
teachWho taught you to drive?
allow / enableThey don't allow people to park here.
get sb to doI got somebody to help me. = уговорил

В пассиве та же схема: I was warned not to touch it. · Are we allowed to park here?

⚠️ Без to: make / let (и help, dare)

make sb doI made him promise he wouldn't tell. не made him to promise
make sb doHot weather makes me feel tired.
let sb doHer parents wouldn't let her go out alone. · Let me carry your bag.
но в пассиве с to!We were made to wait two hours.
help — как хочешьCan you help me move this table? = help me to move…
dareI didn't dare (to) tell him. · I daren't tell him. после daren't — без to

📌 У let нет пассива: вместо него говорят We were allowed to stay.

⚖️

Оба варианта — но смысл разный

remember · forget · regret · go on · try · need · stop · like

🧠 remember / forget

remember doing — помню, что делалI clearly remember locking the door.сначала сделал → потом вспомнил
remember doingI still remember arriving in London for the first time.
remember to doRemember to take your passport tomorrow.
forget doingI'll never forget seeing the sea for the first time.
forget to doHe forgot to switch off the oven.
remember to do — не забыл сделатьI remembered to lock the door, but forgot the windows.сначала вспомнил → потом сделал
forget doingI'll never forget seeing the Alps for the first time.воспоминание
forget to doDon't forget to buy bananas.обязанность

🔑 Формула памяти: -ing смотрит назад (воспоминание), to … смотрит вперёд (задача).

😔 regret · go on · stop

regret doing — жалею о сделанномI regret saying what I said.
regret to say — с сожалением сообщаюWe regret to inform you that… формальное письмо
go on doing — продолжать то жеWe can't go on living like this.
go on to do — перейти к новомуHe went on to talk about foreign policy.
stop doing — перестатьHe stopped smoking two years ago.
stop to do — остановиться, чтобы…He stopped to smoke a cigarette. это не «оба варианта глагола», а цель

🔧 try — «пытаться» или «попробовать способ»

try to do = стараться, прилагать усилияI tried to keep my eyes open, but I couldn't.трудно, может не выйти
try doing = попробовать как экспериментThe printer isn't working. Try pressing the green button.лёгкое действие, вдруг поможет

Сравни: I tried to move the table, but it was too heavy. (не смог)  —  I tried moving the table to the other side. (передвинул, чтобы посмотреть, как будет лучше)

🛠️ need — и пассивный трюк

need to do — мне нужно сделатьI need to charge my phone.
need doing — вещь нуждается в…My phone needs charging. = needs to be charged
needs -ingDoes your suit need cleaning?
needs -ingIt's a difficult problem. It needs thinking about carefully.

То же самое работает с want (разговорно) и require (формально): The grass wants cutting. · This requires checking.

🤝 help / can't help

help (to) doEverybody helped (to) clean up after the party.
help sb doCould you help me carry these boxes upstairs?
can't helpI couldn't help laughing when he fell off the chair.
can't helpShe can't help worrying about her exams.
can't help doingI don't like him, but I can't help feeling sorry for him. = не могу удержаться
couldn't helpShe tried to be serious, but she couldn't help laughing.

❤️ like / love / hate — когда разница есть

О привычных действиях можно и так, и так: I love meeting people = I love to meet people. Но:

существующая ситуация → -ingPaul lives in Berlin now. He likes living there. · Do you like being a student? · I hated working there.
I like doingI like cleaning the kitchen. = мне это в кайф
I like to doI like to clean the kitchen as often as possible. = считаю правильным, хоть и не люблю
enjoy / mind — всегда -ingI enjoy cleaning · I don't mind cleaning. никогда не to
like doingI like working from home two days a week.
love doingShe loves cooking for a crowd.
hate doingI hate being kept waiting.
would like toI'd like to book a table for two.
would love to have doneI'd love to have seen your face!
I like — вообщеI like playing tennis.
I'd like — сейчас хочуI'd like to play tennis today.

would like / love / hate / prefer → всегда to … : I'd love to meet your family.
Сожаление о прошлом: would like to have done — I would like to have seen her again. · I'd love to have gone to the party.

✅ Где разницы НЕТ

После begin, start, continue, intend, bother обе формы равноправны:

It started raining / to rain. He continued working / to work. Don't bother locking / to lock it.

⚠️ Одно ограничение: два -ing подряд не ставят. It's starting to rain (а не starting raining).

🎯

prefer · would prefer · would rather

Три способа сказать «лучше бы…» — и у каждого своя схема

1️⃣ prefer — вкус вообще

prefer sth to sthI prefer this coat to the other one.
prefer doing to doingI prefer driving to travelling by train.
prefer to do rather than doI prefer to drive rather than travel by train.
оба варианта окI prefer to live in the country. = I prefer living in the country.

⚠️ После to в prefer A to B идёт -ing, потому что здесь to — предлог: prefer driving to travel.

2️⃣ would prefer — выбор здесь и сейчас

I'd prefer to do something
выбор«Would you prefer tea or coffee?» — «Coffee, please.»
to + инфинитив«Shall we go by train?» — «I'd prefer to drive.»
rather thanI'd prefer to stay at home tonight rather than go to the cinema.

3️⃣ would rather — голый инфинитив

I'd rather do — без to!
✅I'd rather drive.
❌I'd rather to drive.
отрицаниеI'm tired. I'd rather not go out this evening.
короткий ответ«Do you want to go out?» — «I'd rather not.»
чемI'd rather stay at home tonight than go to the cinema. than + голый инфинитив
вопросWhich would you rather do, go to the cinema or go shopping?

⭐ I'd rather you did — когда действует другой

I'd rather you did it — прошедшая форма, настоящий смысл
я или ты«Who's going to drive?» — «I'd rather you drove.»
сроки«Jack will fix your bike tomorrow.» — «I'd rather he did it today.»
просьба не делатьI'd rather you didn't tell anyone what I said.
короткий ответ«Shall I tell Anna?» — «I'd rather you didn't.»
я самI'd rather make dinner now.
пусть тыI'd rather you made dinner now. не you make

Это та же «нереальная» логика, что у wish и if I did: прошедшая форма = вежливая дистанция, а не прошлое.
📌 Про прошлое: I'd rather you hadn't told him. (жаль, что ты сказал)

🕓 Родня: it's time …

it's time to doIt's time to go home.
it's time sb didIt's time we went home. = пора бы уже
с упрёкомIt's high time you found a job! · It's about time he grew up.

Тот же приём: it's time + past = «давно пора, а всё ещё нет».

🔗

Предлог + -ing

Железное правило: после предлога глагол получает -ing

Правило без исключений: in, for, about, of, at, with, on, before, after, by, without, instead of, in spite of — если после них глагол, он в форме -ing.

interested inAre you interested in working for us?
instead ofHe watched a film instead of studying.
afterAfter finishing the report, she went for a walk.
withoutThey left without saying goodbye.
byYou can save money by cooking at home.
beforeBefore going to bed, I always read for ten minutes.
good atI'm not good at learning languages.
fed up withKate must be fed up with studying.
advantages ofWhat are the advantages of having a car?
thanks forThanks for inviting me.
how aboutHow about meeting for lunch tomorrow?
instead ofGo out instead of sitting at home.
in spite ofAmy went to work in spite of feeling ill.

⏱️ before / after / by / without

before -ingBefore going out, I phoned Sarah. = Before I went out
after -ingWhat did you do after leaving school?
by -ing = каким способомYou can improve your English by reading more.
by -ingThe burglars got in by breaking a window.
by not -ingShe made herself ill by not eating properly.
without -ingWe ran ten kilometres without stopping.
without -ingI said it without thinking.
without sb -ingShe needs to work without people disturbing her.

💀 Ловушка: to тоже бывает предлогом

Если to — предлог (а не часть инфинитива), после него идёт -ing:

look forward toAre you looking forward to going on holiday? не to go
be used toI'm used to living alone.
get used toWe'll get used to the noise / to driving on the left.
object toShe objected to being filmed.
prefer… toI prefer driving to travelling by train.
in addition toIn addition to working full-time, he studies at night.
be committed / devoted toThey're committed to reducing waste.

Проверка: подставь существительное. Если looking forward to the weekend звучит нормально — значит to предлог → нужен -ing.

🎯 be used to vs used to — разные вещи!

be/get used to -ing — привыкI'm used to driving on the left.сейчас мне это уже не странно
used to do — раньше делалI used to drive to work every day.раньше да, сейчас нет
процесс привыканияShe wasn't used to driving on the left → she got used to it → now she is used to it.
с существительнымI'm used to the weather here. · They felt strange because I wasn't used to them.
с человекомShe's used to him being away.
❌I'm used to live alone. → I'm used to living alone.

📚 Глагол + предлог + -ing

approve ofHe doesn't approve of swearing.
decide againstWe decided against moving to London.
dream ofI wouldn't dream of asking them for money.
feel likeI don't feel like going out tonight.
insist onThey insisted on paying for the meal.
succeed inHas Paul succeeded in finding a job?
think of / aboutI'm thinking of buying a house.
apologise forYou should apologise for not telling the truth.
talk aboutWe talked about going to South America.

👥 Глагол + кого-то + предлог + -ing

accuse sb ofHe accused me of telling lies. · … of not telling the truth.
congratulate sb onWe congratulated Lisa on winning first prize.
prevent sb fromWhat prevented you from coming?
stop sb (from)The rain didn't stop us (from) enjoying the holiday.
suspect sb ofNobody suspected the general of being a spy.
thank sb forI thanked everyone for helping me.
в пассивеWe were accused of telling lies. · He was suspected of being a spy.

⚠️ apologise TO somebody FOR something: I apologised to them for keeping them waiting. (не apologised them)

🧰

Конструкции, которые надо знать целиком

it's worth · no point · so that · adjective + to · see somebody do · -ing clauses

💰 there's no point in · it's worth · have difficulty

there's no point in -ingThere's no point in having a car if you never use it.
what's the point of -ingWhat's the point of having a car? внимание: no point IN, но the point OF
it's no use / no goodIt's no use worrying about it. · It's no good trying to persuade me.
it's (not) worth -ingIt's a nice town. It's worth spending a few days there.
worth itWe didn't go to bed — it wasn't worth it.
sth worth -ingThere was nothing worth stealing. · It's a great film, worth seeing.
have trouble/difficulty -ingI had no trouble finding a place to stay. не trouble to find
have a problem -ingDid you have a problem getting a visa?
spend / waste time -ingHe spent hours trying to repair the clock. · I waste time doing nothing.
be busy -ingShe was too busy doing other things.
go + -ing (спорт)go swimming / skiing / shopping / jogging / camping / sailing / hiking — We went skiing last year.

🎯 Цель: to … / for … / so that …

to + глагол = зачемI called the restaurant to reserve a table.
for + существительноеWe stopped for petrol.  /  We stopped to get petrol.
for sb to doThere weren't any chairs for us to sit on.
for -ing = для чего вещьThis brush is for washing the dishes. · What's this switch for?
❌ не для причиныI went into the kitchen to wash the dishes. не for washing
so that + can/willShe's learning English so that she can study in Canada.
so that + could/wouldI hurried so that I wouldn't be late.
место/вещь + to doa place to park · something to eat · work to do · a chair to sit on · somebody to talk to

🔑 Разница: to do = зачем ты это делаешь; for + сущ. = ради чего; so that = когда нужен другой субъект или модальный глагол.

🔤 Adjective + to …

через itIt is hard to understand him.
через подлежащееHe is hard to understand. не hard to understand him
easy / hard / impossibleThe questions were impossible to answer. не to answer them
safe / dangerousIs this water safe to drink?
interesting / niceNicola is interesting to talk to.
+ существительноеThis is a difficult question to answer.
it's nice OF sb TO doIt was nice of you to take me to the airport.
того же типаkind / generous / careless / silly / stupid / unfair of somebody to do
реакцияI'm sorry to hear that. · Was Julia surprised to see you? · We were glad to get home.
после the first / onlyI was the only one to arrive on time. · You'll be the first to know.
sure / bound / likelyShe's bound to pass the exam. · It's not likely to happen.

😨 afraid / interested / sorry — две схемы, два смысла

afraid to do — боюсь и не делаюPeople are afraid to walk here at night.сознательно не идут
afraid of doing — боюсь, что случитсяThe path was icy. We were afraid of falling.боимся последствия
interested in doing — хочу этим занятьсяLet me know if you're interested in joining the club.
interested to hear — было любопытно узнатьI was interested to hear that Tanya left her job.
sorry for doing — извиняюсь за поступокI'm sorry for shouting at you yesterday.
sorry to hear — сочувствую / жальI'm sorry to hear that Nicky lost her job.

Ещё: I'm sorry to bother you, but… — извинение в момент действия. И I'll be sorry to leave.

👀 see somebody do / see somebody doing

do — видел действие целикомI saw Tom get into his car and drive off.от начала до конца
doing — застал в процессеI saw Kate waiting for a bus.кусок действия
hear sb doI didn't hear you come in. · I heard them go out.
hear sth doingI could hear it raining. · Listen to the birds singing!
feelLisa suddenly felt somebody touch her on the shoulder.
smell / find / watchCan you smell something burning? · We found him sitting under a tree.

⚠️ После see/hear/feel/watch — инфинитив без to: I saw him to get in. Но в пассиве to возвращается: He was seen to leave the building.

📖 -ing clauses — два действия в одном предложении

одновременноKate is in the kitchen making coffee. · A man ran out shouting.
во время чего-тоJoe hurt his knee playing football. = while he was playing
while / when + -ingBe careful when crossing the road. · He hurt himself while playing.
причинаFeeling tired, I went to bed early. = because I felt tired
отрицательная причинаNot having a car, she finds it hard to get around.
раньше → having doneHaving finished her work, she went home. · Having seen the film twice, I didn't want to see it again.
after -ingAfter finishing her work, she went home.

✏️ Если предложение начинается с -ing части — после неё нужна запятая. Это письменный стиль: в разговоре чаще скажут because… / after she…

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. -ing или to …?

1. I enjoy cooking (cook) at the weekend.
2. They decided not to go (not / go) out.
3. Would you mind opening (open) the window?
4. He managed to fix (fix) the printer himself.
5. I can't afford to buy (buy) a new laptop.
6. She suggested meeting (meet) at eight.
7. Do you fancy going (go) for a walk?
8. He denied breaking (break) the window.
9. I'm looking forward to seeing (see) you again.
10. She's used to working (work) at night.
11. We used to live (live) in Prague.
12. It's no use arguing (argue) with him.

B. Выбери форму по смыслу

1. I remember locking the door — я точно помню, как закрыл.
2. Remember to lock the door when you leave — не забудь.
3. The printer's stuck. Try pressing the green button — попробуй так.
4. I tried to lift the box, but it was too heavy.
5. My bike needs repairing — велосипед нуждается в ремонте.
6. I need to repair my bike this weekend.
7. He stopped smoking last year — бросил.
8. On the way home he stopped to buy milk — остановился, чтобы купить.
9. We can't go on living like this.
10. After the intro, she went on to explain the results.

C. Скажи вежливее: would rather / would prefer

1. Я бы лучше остался дома. → I'd rather stay at home. / I'd prefer to stay at home.
2. Я бы предпочёл, чтобы ты не рассказывал ей. → I'd rather you didn't tell her.
3. Лучше поехать поездом, чем лететь. → I'd rather go by train than fly. / I'd prefer to go by train rather than fly.
4. Я вообще предпочитаю чай кофе. → I prefer tea to coffee.
5. Пора бы тебе найти работу. → It's time you found a job.

D. Найди и исправь ошибку

1. I'm looking forward to see you. → … to seeing you.
2. She suggested me to take a taxi. → She suggested taking a taxi. / She suggested (that) I take a taxi.
3. I'm used to live alone. → I'm used to living alone.
4. My parents didn't let me to go. → … didn't let me go.
5. I'd rather to walk. → I'd rather walk.
6. It's difficult to answer this question it. → This question is difficult to answer.
7. Thanks for to help me. → Thanks for helping me.
8. I apologised them for being late. → I apologised to them for being late.
9. He accused me to steal his phone. → He accused me of stealing his phone.
10. I saw him to leave the building. → I saw him leave the building.
11. There's no point to wait. → There's no point in waiting.
12. I had trouble to find the address. → I had trouble finding the address.
