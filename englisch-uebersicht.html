Überblick & Diagnose

Все времена в одной таблице · 7 форм будущего · модальные по функциям · тест на 40 вопросов, который сам скажет, что повторить

Зачем эта страница. На сайте есть отдельные страницы почти по каждому времени и модальному глаголу — но не было сводной картины и не было способа быстро понять, что именно у тебя проседает. Здесь три обзорные таблицы (времена, будущее, модальные) и диагностика на 40 вопросов: после каждой ошибки она даёт ссылку на нужную страницу сайта, а в конце собирает список тем для повторения.

Матрица: simple × continuous, present × past

Вся система английских времён держится на двух вопросах: факт или процесс? (simple / continuous) и когда точка отсчёта? (сейчас / в прошлом / до какого-то момента).

Simple — факт, сколько разContinuous — процесс, как долго
Present I do
Anna plays tennis twice a week.
The shop doesn't open on Sundays.
Water boils at 100°.		 I am doing
Anna is playing tennis right now.
Don't call me, I'm working.
Prices are rising again.
Present perfect I have done
She has played there many times.
I've lost my keys — result now.
We've known each other for years.		 I have been doing
She's tired — she's been playing tennis.
How long have you been learning German?
It's been raining all day.
Past I did
She played tennis yesterday.
I lost my keys on Monday.
The train left at six.		 I was doing
At six she was playing tennis.
I was cooking when he called.
It was raining when we left.
Past perfect I had done
The match had finished before we arrived.
I realised I had left the keys inside.
It was the first time I had flown.		 I had been doing
She was tired — she had been playing for hours.
The ground was wet: it had been raining.
How long had you been waiting?

Что выбрать: три быстрых теста

1. Сколько раз?Есть число или «сколько раз» → simple: I've read it twice. She wrote three emails.
2. Как долго?Есть «сколько времени / с каких пор» → continuous: I've been waiting since two.
3. Время закончилось?Указан конченный отрезок (yesterday, in 2019, last week) → past simple, перфект невозможен.
Осторожноknow, like, want, need, believe, belong, own — без continuous: ❌ I'm knowing → ✅ I know.
Исключениеbe и have меняют смысл: He is being rude (ведёт себя). I'm having lunch (ем).

Сигнальные слова

ВремяСлова-маркеры

Present continuousnow, at the moment, today, this week, currently, these days
Present perfectjust, already, yet, ever, never, so far, recently, this week, since, for
Present perfect cont.how long, since, for, all day, all morning
Past simpleyesterday, last night, in 2019, two years ago, when I was a child
Past continuousat eight o'clock, while, when (фон для другого действия)

Маркеры помогают, но не решают: this week живёт и с перфектом (I've read two books this week), и с continuous (I'm working from home this week).

Самые частые ошибки в системе времён

❌ Так не говорят
  • I live here since 2019.
  • I have seen him yesterday.
  • When have you arrived?
  • I am knowing the answer.
  • She is working here since May.
✅ Правильно
  • I've lived here since 2019.
  • I saw him yesterday.
  • When did you arrive?
  • I know the answer.
  • She's been working here since May.

ФормаКогдаПример

future perfect		будет завершено к моментуBy June I'll have finished the course.
before I leave
present после when/if/before		после when, if, before, after, until, as soon as — настоящееI'll call you when I get home. ❌ when I will get

will или going to — главное различие

going to — решил ДО разговораI'm going to sell my car. (решил вчера)
will — решаю В момент речи— The car's making a noise. — OK, I'll sell it.
going to — вижу признакиShe's going to have a baby.
will — думаю, предполагаюI think it'll be a difficult year.
предложениеI'll carry that for you. — спонтанная помощь, только will
обещаниеI won't tell anyone, I promise.
отказThe car won't start. — won't = отказывается
was going toI was going to call you, but I forgot. — собирался и не сделал
be about toHurry — the film is about to start. — вот-вот
shallShall I open the window? Shall we go? — только предложения и в вопросе с I/we

Модальные: одна таблица на все функции

ФункцияНастоящее / будущееПрошлое
Способностьcan swim · is able tocould swim (вообще) · was able to (в конкретный раз)
Разрешениеcan / may / could I…?was allowed to

Ключевые различия, на которых сыпятся

mustn't ≠ don't have toYou mustn't park here (нельзя) / You don't have to park here (можно не).
must ≠ have toMust — я сам считаю нужным; have to — правило извне. I must stop smoking. / I have to wear a uniform.
could ≠ was able toI could swim at five (умение) / The water was cold but I was able to swim across (один раз получилось).
should haveYou should have told me. — надо было, но не сделал
can't haveShe can't have forgotten — she wrote it down. — отрицание догадки, не mustn't
wouldI would take the job, but the pay is terrible. — воображаемая ситуация
would (прошлое)Every summer we would go to the lake. = used to, о повторявшемся

Формы: что после модального

modal + infinitive без to  ·  modal + have + V3 (прошлое)  ·  modal + be + -ing (сейчас/процесс)
+ infShe might come later. ❌ might to come
+ have V3She might have come while we were out.
+ be -ingHe must be joking. · They may be waiting outside.
нет -s❌ He cans / musts → ✅ He can, he must.
два модальных нельзя❌ will can → ✅ will be able to; ❌ must can → ✅ have to be able to.

Тренировка

Ответ спрятан — кликни по серому прямоугольнику

A. Времена: поставь глагол в нужную форму

1. I have known (know) her since 2018.
2. Look at the sky — it is going to rain (rain).
3. When I got home, the film had already started (already / start).
4. She's exhausted — she has been working (work) since six.
5. What were you doing (you / do) at nine last night?
6. The train leaves (leave) at 7.40 tomorrow.
7. By next summer I will have lived (live) here for ten years.
8. I saw (see) that film last week — it's good.
9. This time tomorrow we will be sitting (sit) on the beach.
10. Don't phone at eight — I will be having (have) dinner.

B. Будущее: will / going to / present continuous

1. — The bags look heavy. — I'll help you.
2. We 're meeting Anna at seven — it's arranged.
3. I've decided: I 'm going to learn Spanish this year.
4. I think prices will rise again in autumn.
5. Call me as soon as you arrive (arrive). (не «will arrive»)
6. I was going to phone you, but my battery died.

C. Модальные

1. You mustn't use your phone here. (запрещено)
2. You don't have to come if you're busy. (не обязательно)
3. The water was freezing, but she was able to swim to the boat.
4. You should have told me earlier — I'd have helped.
5. He can't have seen us — he was facing the other way.
6. Next year I will be able to drive. (не «will can»)
7. It's late — we had better leave now.
8. You needn't have paid — the tickets were free. (заплатил зря)
