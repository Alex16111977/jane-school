so · such · enough · too · quite · even

Слова, которые усиливают и уточняют: so tired vs such a nice day · too / enough · quite / pretty / rather / fairly · still / yet / already / any more · even

💥

so и such

Одно значение — «такой / настолько» — но разные соседи

so + прилагательное / наречие
  • The story was so stupid.
  • Everything happened so quickly.
  • He talks so quietly.
  • I'm so tired.
such + (прилагательное) + существительное
  • It was such a stupid story.
  • They are such nice people.
  • We had such a good time.
  • You're such an optimist.

❌ a so stupid story  ❌ so nice people  ❌ a such big dog
✅ Артикль идёт после such: such a big dog.

🔗 so … that / such … that

so … thatI was so tired that I fell asleep in the armchair.
such … thatIt was such nice weather that we spent the whole day on the beach.
без thatI was so tired I fell asleep. · It was such nice weather we stayed out all day.
so + прилагательноеThe exam was so easy that I finished in twenty minutes.
so + наречиеHe speaks so fast that nobody understands him.
such + сущ.It was such a long journey that we slept most of the way.
such + мн.ч.They're such nice neighbours — we're lucky.
so much / so manyThere was so much noise that I couldn't hear the announcement.

В разговоре that обычно выбрасывают — смысл не меняется.

🔍 Второе значение: «настолько / такой вот»

so = как естьI didn't realise it was so old. = настолько старый
such = такой вотI didn't realise it was such an old house.
such a thingHow can you say such a thing? = вещь вроде этой
no such …There's no such word in English. = такого слова нет

⚖️ Пары, где легко ошибиться

so longI haven't seen her for so long.
such a long time… for such a long time. не so long time
so farI didn't know it was so far.
such a long way… it was such a long way.
so much / so manyThere was so much traffic.
such a lot ofThere was such a lot of traffic.
📏

enough и too

«Хватает» — и «слишком»

Место enough зависит от того, что рядом:

СхемаПример
прилагательное/наречие + enoughI'm not fit enough. · We've waited long enough.
enough + существительноеWe have enough money. · There weren't enough chairs.
enough само по себеWe don't need more — we have enough.

❌ enough fit · enough long — после прилагательного, всегда.

⚖️ too … / not … enough — две стороны одной медали

too = больше, чем нужноYou work too hard.
not enough = меньше, чем нужноYou don't work hard enough.
too much / too manyThere's too much furniture in this room.
not enoughThere's not enough space.
too manyThere were too many people…
not enough… and not enough chairs.

⚠️ too ≠ very. It's very hot = жарко (и это может быть хорошо). It's too hot = жарко сверх меры, это проблема.

🔗 enough / too + for … и to …

for + кто/чтоDoes Joe have enough experience for the job?
for + кто/чтоThat shirt is too small for you.
to + глаголDoes Joe have enough experience to do the job?
to + глаголIt's too far to walk home from here.
to + глаголShe's not old enough to have a driving licence.
и то, и другоеThe bridge is just wide enough for two cars to pass.
not old enoughHe's not old enough to vote yet.
warm enoughIs it warm enough to sit outside?
too + прилаг.The music was too loud to talk over.
too muchThere's too much sugar in this coffee.
enough + сущ.We don't have enough chairs for everyone.

💀 too hot to eat — без второго «it»

✅The food was too hot to eat.
❌too hot to eat it
✅These boxes are too heavy to carry.
❌too heavy to carry them
✅This chair isn't strong enough to stand on.
❌strong enough to stand on it

Предмет уже назван в начале предложения — повторять его местоимением нельзя. Ср. с равнозначным: The food was so hot that we couldn't eat it. (тут it нужен!)

🎚️

quite · pretty · rather · fairly

Все переводятся как «довольно» — но сила разная

СловоСилаОттенок
fairlyслабее всех«ничего так, но могло быть и лучше»
quiteсредненейтрально: больше, чем «немного», меньше, чем «очень»
pretty= quiteразговорное, в устной речи
rather= quiteчаще о том, что не нравится; с плюсом = «на удивление»
fairlyMy room is fairly big, but I'd prefer a bigger one.
quite / prettyShe's quite famous. = She's pretty famous.
rather — минусThe weather is rather cloudy. · Paul is rather shy.
rather — плюсThese oranges are rather good! = неожиданно хорошие

📍 quite + артикль — порядок слов

✅ quite перед a/anWe live in quite an old house.
❌a quite old house
но pretty — после артикляSarah has a pretty good job. = quite a good job
rather — можно и так, и такrather a long journey = a rather long journey
quite a/an + сущ.It was quite a surprise. = довольно большой сюрприз
quite a lot (of)There were quite a lot of guests at the wedding.
quite + глаголI quite like tennis, but it's not my favourite. · I quite enjoyed the film.

Такие схемы есть только у quite: pretty a surprise, I pretty like it.

⭐ Второе значение quite = «полностью»

С «абсолютными» прилагательными quite означает не «довольно», а «совершенно»:

quite surequite certainquite right quite wrongquite truequite safe quite clearquite obviousquite different quite impossiblequite amazingquite unnecessary
quite = довольноThe story is quite interesting.
quite = совершенноThe story is quite true.
с глаголомI quite agree with you. = полностью согласен
not quiteI don't quite understand what you mean. · «Ready?» — «Not quite.»
🕐

still · yet · already · any more

Четыре слова о том, изменилась ситуация или нет

СловоСмыслМесто
stillвсё ещё продолжаетсяв середине (перед смысловым глаголом)
not … any more / any longerбольше нев конце
no longerбольше не (книжнее)в середине
yetещё нет / уже? (ожидаем)в конце
alreadyуже (раньше, чем ждали)в середине или в конце

🔄 still и not … any more

stillIt's ten o'clock and Joe is still in bed.
stillDo you still want to go, or have you changed your mind?
still = «и всё же»He has everything he needs, but he's still unhappy.
not any moreLucy doesn't work here any more. She left last month.
not any longerWe used to be friends, but we aren't any longer.
no longerLucy no longer works here. формальнее
❌We are no more friends. → We are no longer friends. / We aren't friends any more.

Сравни: Sally still works here, but Lucy doesn't work here any more.

⏳ yet vs still (в отрицаниях)

yet — нейтральное «ещё не»He hasn't replied yet.жду, ответит скоро
still not — с раздражениемHe still hasn't replied.давно пора было ответить!
yet в отрицанииIt's 10 o'clock and Joe isn't here yet.
yet в вопросеHave you decided what to do yet?
короткий ответ«Where are you going?» — «We don't know yet.»
still + процессIs it still raining? — Has it stopped raining yet?
пара примеровMike lost his job six months ago and is still unemployed. / … and hasn't found another job yet.

✅ already — «уже, раньше, чем ждали»

раньше ожидания«What time is Sue leaving?» — «She has already left.»
уже в курсеShall I tell Joe, or does he already know?
удивлениеI've just had lunch and I'm already hungry.
stillIt's June and it's still cold here.
yetHave you finished your homework yet?
not any moreHe doesn't play football any more — his knee is bad.
no longerThis model is no longer available.
still + отрицаниеI ordered it three weeks ago and it still hasn't arrived.
местоShe's already left. = She's left already.
‼️

even

«Даже» — сигнал «это необычно / неожиданно»

🌟 even + слово, которое удивляет

неожиданное местоShe has a TV in every room, even the bathroom.
even + человекEveryone laughed, even the teacher.
even + времяThe shop is open every day, even on Sundays.
not evenHe didn't even look at the menu.
even + сравнениеToday is even hotter than yesterday.
неожиданный человекThese photos are awful. Even I take better pictures.
неожиданные условияHe always wears a coat, even in hot weather.
not evenNobody would help her, not even her best friend.

📍 even рядом с глаголом — в середине

после вспомогательногоShe's travelled everywhere. She's even been to the Antarctic.
перед смысловымThey're very rich. They even have their own jet.
can't evenI can't cook. I can't even boil an egg.
didn't evenThey weren't friendly. They didn't even say hello.
not even + прилаг.She's been running fast and she's not even out of breath.

📈 even + сравнительная степень = «ещё более»

even + -erI got up early, but Jack got up even earlier.
even lessI knew I didn't have much money, but I have even less than I thought.
even moreWe were surprised by her email — and even more surprised when she came round.

Тот же смысл дают much / far / a lot / any + сравнительная: much better, far worse, a lot cheaper.

⚠️ even though / even when / even if — когда дальше идёт предложение

❌ even + подлежащее + глагол
  • Even she can't drive, she has a car.
  • I can't reach it even I stand on a chair.
  • even you are a strong swimmer
✅ even though / even if / even when
  • Even though she can't drive, she has a car.
  • I can't reach it even if I stand on a chair.
  • It's dangerous even if you're a strong swimmer.
  • He never shouts, even when he's angry.
even though — фактEven though Tina can't drive, she has a car.она правда не умеет
even if — гипотезаWe're going even if the weather is bad.погода ещё неизвестна

И сравни с обычным if: We won't go if the weather is bad. (тогда не поедем) — We're going even if the weather is bad. (поедем в любом случае)

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. so или such?

1. It was _____ a boring film that I left. → such
2. The film was _____ boring that I left. → so
3. They were _____ nice people. → such
4. I've never seen _____ a big dog. → such
5. Why are you walking _____ fast? → so
6. There was _____ a lot of traffic. → such
7. I've been waiting for _____ a long time. → such
8. I didn't know the museum was _____ far away. → so

B. too или enough? Поставь слово на место

1. (old / not) He's _____ to drive. → not old enough
2. (small) This flat is _____ for five people. → too small
3. (time / not) We don't have _____ to finish. → enough time
4. (hot) The coffee was _____ to drink. → too hot — без «it» в конце!
5. (loud) You're speaking _____ — I can't hear you. → not loudly enough
6. (people) There were _____ and not enough seats. → too many people

C. quite, rather, fairly, pretty

1. We live in _____ old house. → quite an
2. Are you sure? — Yes, _____ sure. → quite (= совершенно)
3. It's _____ cold today — take a jacket. (с оттенком «неприятно») → rather
4. My German is _____ good, but far from perfect. → fairly
5. I _____ like jazz, but I don't love it. → quite
6. That's _____ impossible. → quite (= абсолютно)

D. still, yet, already, any more, even

1. He left an hour ago, but she's _____ waiting. → still
2. Have you finished _____? → yet
3. I don't smoke _____. I stopped in March. → any more / any longer
4. It's only 7 am and he has _____ gone to work. → already
5. I wrote to her a month ago and she _____ hasn't replied. → still
6. He didn't _____ say goodbye. → even
7. _____ though it was raining, we went out. → Even
8. She _____ works there — she moved to Berlin. → no longer
