Союзы и предлоги-связки

although vs in spite of · in case · unless / as long as · as / like / as if · during / while · by / until

🔀

although · though · even though · in spite of · despite

Все значат «хотя / несмотря на» — но грамматика у них разная

Правило, которое решает 90 % ошибок:

although / though / even though
  • + подлежащее и глагол
  • Although it rained, they had a good time.
  • Although the traffic was bad, we arrived on time.
in spite of / despite
  • + существительное, местоимение или -ing
  • In spite of the rain, they had a good time.
  • Despite being very tired, I couldn't sleep.

❌ In spite of the traffic was bad…  ❌ Despite I was tired…  ❌ despite of — despite идёт без of!

🧰 Как переделать одно в другое

although + предложениеin spite of + существительное/-ing
Although it rained a lot…In spite of the rain…
Although I was tired…Despite being tired… / In spite of my tiredness…
Although she wasn't well, she kept working.Despite this, she kept working.
Although I had the qualifications…In spite of having the qualifications…

🔑 Мост между схемами: in spite of the fact (that) / despite the fact (that) + предложение:
I didn't apply despite the fact that I had the qualifications.

⚖️ although vs because — проверка смысла

although — вопрекиWe went out although it was raining heavily.
because — причинаWe didn't go out because it was raining heavily.
in spite of + сущ.We went out in spite of the rain.
because of + сущ.We didn't go out because of the rain.

🗣️ though — разговорный трюк

= althoughThough I had the qualifications, I didn't apply.
в конце = «зато / но»The house isn't so nice. I like the garden though.
в концеI see them every day. I've never spoken to them though.
even though — сильнееEven though I was really tired, I couldn't sleep.
❌Even I was tired… — одно even так не работает.

💡 though в конце фразы — очень частый разговорный ход, эквивалент нашего «правда, …» или «зато».

🔗 Соседи по смыслу

whereas / whileHe's very tidy, whereas his brother is a mess. — противопоставление двух вещей
howeverIt rained all week. However, we had a great time. — новое предложение + запятая
neverthelessThe plan was risky. Nevertheless, they went ahead. формально
no matter howNo matter how hard I try, I can't remember his name.
even ifI'm going even if it rains. — условие, а не факт: дождя может и не быть

⚠️ even though = факт («хотя на самом деле…»), even if = гипотеза («даже если вдруг…»).

🔑

in case · unless · as long as · provided

Четыре способа поставить условие — и они не взаимозаменяемы

🛡️ in case — «на случай, если»

делаю что-то сейчас, потому что потом может понадобиться
запасное колесоYour car should have a spare wheel in case you have a puncture.
парольI'd better write down my password in case I forget it.
картаShall I draw a map in case you have a problem finding our house?
напоминаниеI'll remind them in case they've forgotten.
just in caseI don't think it'll rain, but I'll take an umbrella just in case.
в прошломI gave him my number in case he needed to contact me.
in case of + сущ.In case of fire, leave the building. = if there is a fire — язык табличек

⭐ in case ≠ if — главное различие

in case — покупаю заранееWe'll buy more food in case Tom comes.купим сейчас в любом случае — вдруг он придёт
if — куплю только еслиWe'll buy more food if Tom comes.не придёт — не купим
in caseI'll give you my number in case you need to contact me.
ifCall me on this number if you need to contact me.
in caseInsure your bike in case it's stolen.
ifInform the police if your bike is stolen.

❌ После in case нет will: in case I will forget → in case I forget.

🚪 unless = except if

клубYou can't go in unless you are a member. = только если ты член клуба
планыI'll see you tomorrow unless I have to work late.
короткий ответ«Shall I tell Lisa?» — «Not unless she asks you.»
= if … notUnless we leave now, we'll be late. = If we don't leave now…

⚠️ После unless нет второго отрицания: unless we don't leave. Само unless уже содержит «не».

🤝 as long as · so long as · provided (that) · providing (that)

Все означают «при условии, что» — выдвигают требование.

as long asYou can borrow my car as long as you promise not to drive too fast.
so long asI don't mind the noise so long as it stops by ten.
provided (that)Travelling by car is convenient provided (that) you have somewhere to park.
providing (that)Providing the room is clean, I don't mind which hotel we stay at.
on condition thatThey let him go on condition that he reported to the police weekly. очень формально

⚠️ Общее правило: никакого will после этих союзов

❌
  • unless it will stop raining
  • providing the weather will be good
  • in case I will forget
  • while I will be there
  • by the time we will arrive
✅
  • unless it stops raining
  • providing the weather is good
  • in case I forget
  • while I am there
  • by the time we arrive

Тот же запрет действует после if, when, as soon as, until, before, after, once. Будущее выражает только главная часть.

🎭

as · like · as if

Самая путаная тройка в английском

⏱️ as = «в тот момент, когда» (одновременность)

два действия сразуWe all waved goodbye as she drove away.
фонAs I walked along the street, I looked in the shop windows.
просьбаCan you turn off the light as you go out?
во время процессаKate slipped as she was getting off the bus.
just as = ровно в тот мигJust as I sat down, the doorbell rang.
параллельное развитиеAs the day went on, the weather got worse.
as — одновременноAs I drove home, I listened to music.
when — сначала одно, потом другоеWhen I got home, I had something to eat. не as I got home

💡 as = because

причинаAs I was hungry, I decided to find somewhere to eat.
причинаAs it's late and we're tired, let's get a taxi.
since = asSince it's late, let's get a taxi home.
as = потому чтоDavid wasn't in the office as he was on holiday.
when = в то времяDavid lost his passport when he was on holiday.

📌 as и since в значении «потому что» ставят обычно в начале и звучат мягче, чем because — причина подаётся как уже известная.

⚖️ like vs as

like = похоже на (предлог)
  • + существительное: It's like a palace.
  • + местоимение: I hate weather like this.
  • + -ing: It's like walking on ice.
  • It sounds like a baby crying.
  • = например: water sports, like surfing — или such as surfing
as = так же, как (+ подлежащее и глагол)
  • I left everything as it was.
  • You should have done it as I showed you.
  • as usual / as always: You're late as usual.
  • the same as: Your phone is the same as mine.
  • as you know / as I said / as he expected

🗣️ В разговоре часто говорят like I showed you, like I said — это нормально, но на письме безопаснее as.
❌ А вот as this вместо like this — ошибка всегда: Do it like this.

🎬 as + существительное — «в роли, в качестве»

as = я реально им являюсьAs a taxi driver, I spend my life in a car.я действительно таксист
like = я похож на негоEveryone wants me to drive them. I'm like a taxi driver.я не таксист
работаI worked as a photographer for two years.
функцияMany words can be used as verbs or nouns.
в качествеLondon is fine as a place to visit, but I wouldn't live there.
устойчивоThe news came as a great shock.

👀 as if / as though / like — «как будто»

lookThat house looks as if it's going to fall down. = looks like it's going to…
soundAmy sounded as if she had a cold.
feelI don't feel as though I've had a holiday.
it looks like…Sarah's very late. It looks like she isn't coming.
it sounds as if…The noise is loud. It sounds as if they're having a party.
с другими глаголамиHe ran as if he was running for his life. · They looked at me as though I was mad.
look + прилагательноеYou look tired.
look as if + предложениеYou look as if you haven't slept.

🔮 as if + прошедшее = «это неправда»

он не всё знаетHe talks as if he knew everything. — но он не знает
мне и так есть чем занятьсяShe keeps asking me — as if I didn't have enough to do.
ему всего 40Why do you talk about him as if he was/were an old man?
я им не сынThey treat me as if I were their own son.

Та же логика, что у wish и if I did: прошедшее время = «на самом деле это не так». С like так обычно не говорят.

🕓

during · for · while · by · until

Когда — сколько — до какого момента

СловоЧто после негоЧто означает
duringсуществительноеКОГДА: в течение чего-то
forотрезок времениСКОЛЬКО: как долго
whileподлежащее + глаголКОГДА: пока происходило
byмомент времениК моменту, не позже
until / tillмомент / предложениеДО какого момента длится

📅 during vs for

during — когда?I fell asleep during the movie.«When did you fall asleep?» — «During the movie.»
for — как долго?I slept for half an hour.«How long were you asleep?» — «For half an hour.»
during + событиеWe met nice people during our holiday. · It must have rained during the night.
in = duringIt rained in the night = during the night. · It's lovely here in / during the summer.
for + длительностьWe watched TV for two hours. · I haven't seen you for ages.
❌It rained during three days → It rained for three days.

🔄 during vs while

during + существительноеI fell asleep during the movie.
while + предложениеI fell asleep while I was watching TV.
duringWe met people during our holiday.
whileWe met people while we were on holiday.
whileWe saw Clare while we were waiting for the bus.
whileWhile you were out, there was a phone call for you.
будущееI hope the weather is good while I'm there. не while I will be
while + -ingHe hurt his knee while playing football. краткая форма

⏳ by vs until

by = не позже чемJoe will be back by Monday.в понедельник или раньше
until = вплоть доJoe will be away until Monday.до понедельника его нет
byI'll have finished by 11.30.закончу не позже 11:30
untilI'll be working until 11.30.работаю до 11:30
by + срокThe documents should arrive by Monday. · We have to be home by 5.
by nowWhere's Sarah? She should be here by now.
until + предложениеLet's wait until it stops raining. till = until, разговорнее
not … untilI didn't get up until half past ten.

⭐ by the time …

= «к тому моменту, когда…». Часто рядом стоит Future Perfect или Past Perfect.

будущееBy the time we get there, it will be closed.
будущееBy the time we get to the cinema, the film will have started.
прошлоеBy the time she arrived, most of the guests had left.
прошлоеI was very tired by the time I finished.
by then / by that timeShe got there at midnight, but by then most guests had left.

⚠️ После by the time о будущем — настоящее время: by the time we will get there.

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. although или in spite of / despite?

1. _____ the heavy rain, the match went ahead. → In spite of / Despite
2. _____ it was raining heavily, the match went ahead. → Although / Even though
3. She got the job _____ having no experience. → in spite of / despite
4. She got the job _____ she had no experience. → although / even though
5. We didn't go out _____ the rain. → because of
6. I couldn't sleep _____ I was exhausted. → although / even though
7. He passed the exam _____ the fact that he had hardly studied. → despite / in spite of

B. in case, if, unless, as long as

1. Take an umbrella _____ it rains. (берёшь заранее) → in case
2. We'll cancel the picnic _____ it rains. (только тогда) → if
3. You won't pass _____ you study harder. → unless
4. You can stay _____ you keep the place tidy. → as long as / provided (that)
5. _____ fire, break the glass. → In case of
6. I'll wait here _____ you get back. → until
7. I wrote it down _____ I forgot. (в прошлом) → in case

C. as, like или as if?

1. This soup tastes _____ chicken. → like
2. Do it _____ I showed you. → as (разг. like)
3. You're late, _____ usual. → as
4. He worked _____ a waiter for two years. → as
5. It looks _____ it's going to snow. → as if / like
6. My sister is nothing _____ me. → like
7. He behaves _____ he owned the place. → as if / as though (+ past)
8. _____ I was tired, I went to bed early. → As / Since (= because)

D. during, for, while, by, until

1. I lived in Poland _____ three years. → for
2. I learned a lot _____ my time in Poland. → during
3. I learned a lot _____ I was living in Poland. → while
4. The shop stays open _____ 10 pm. → until / till
5. Send it to me _____ Friday at the latest. → by
6. _____ we arrived, everyone had gone home. → By the time
7. Someone called _____ you were out. → while
8. He should be here _____ now. → by
