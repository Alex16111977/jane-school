Предлоги: тонкости

on time или in time · at the end или in the end · in the corner или on the corner · to / into · by car, in a taxi

🕓

at / on / in — время

Точка → день → период

ПредлогЧто после негоПримеры
atвремя суток, точкаat 5 o'clock · at 11.45 · at midnight · at lunchtime · at sunset
onдни и датыon Friday · on Fridays · on 16 May · on New Year's Day · on my birthday
inдлинные периодыin June · in 2012 · in the 1990s · in the 20th century · in winter · in the past

📌 Устойчивые сочетания с at

at the momentCan we talk later? I'm busy at the moment. = at present, at this time
at the same timeKate and I arrived at the same time.
at the weekendWill you be here at the weekend? AmE: on the weekend
at ChristmasDo you give presents at Christmas? но: on Christmas Day
at night vs in the nightI don't like working at night. вообще по ночам · I was woken by a noise in the night. той конкретной ночью

🌅 in the morning или on Friday morning?

in — просто утроI'll see you in the morning. · Do you work in the evenings?
on — утро конкретного дняI'll see you on Friday morning. · Do you work on Saturday evenings?

Логика: как только появляется день недели, включается его предлог on.

⚠️ Перед last / next / this / every предлога НЕТ

❌
  • on next Friday
  • in last June
  • on this Monday
  • in every summer
✅
  • I'll see you next Friday.
  • They got married last June.
  • See you this Monday.
  • We go there every summer.

🗣️ А ещё в разговоре on перед днями часто просто опускают: I'll see you Friday. · I don't work Monday mornings.

⏳ in + отрезок = «через» и «за»

через сколькоThe train will be leaving in a few minutes. = через несколько минут
через сколькоHe'll be back in a week. · They'll be here in a moment.
за сколькоI learnt to drive in four weeks. = обучение заняло 4 недели
at + времяThe meeting starts at half past nine.
on + деньWe're flying on 12 August.
in + периодShe was born in the 1990s.
in + сезонIt gets dark early in winter.
on + утро дняI have a dentist appointment on Monday morning.

Не путай с for (как долго длилось) и during (когда): I studied for four weeks. · It happened during the lesson.

⚖️

Пары, на которых все спотыкаются

on time / in time · at the end / in the end

on time = вовремя, по расписаниюThe 11.45 train left on time.ровно в 11:45, не опоздал
in time = успеть, до срокаWill you be home in time for dinner?достаточно рано, чтобы успеть
on timePlease be on time. Don't be late. · Everything began and finished on time.
in time forI hope the present arrives in time for her birthday.
in time to doI want to get home in time to watch the game.
just in timeA child ran into the road, but I stopped just in time. = чуть не поздно
on timeThe lecture began on time, at exactly ten.
in timeWe got to the gate in time for boarding.
in time toI came home in time to see the second half.
too lateHe arrived too late to say goodbye.
противоположностьI got home too late to watch the game.

🏁 at the end vs in the end

at the end (of…) = в момент окончанияAt the end of the concert, everyone applauded.противоположность: at the beginning
in the end = в итоге, наконецWe had problems with the car. We sold it in the end.противоположность: at first
at the end ofat the end of the month / of January / of the film / of the course / of the game
❌in the end of January — так не говорят никогда
in the endHe got angrier and angrier. In the end he just walked out.
параAt first we didn't get on, but in the end we became good friends.
📍

in / at / on — место

Внутри · точка · поверхность — и десятки устойчивых сочетаний

📦 in = внутри чего-то
  • in a room / a building / a garden
  • in a box / in your hand / in your mouth
  • in a town / in Italy / in the city centre
  • in the sea / in a river / in the pool
📌 at = точка
  • at the bus stop / at the door / at the window
  • at the traffic lights / at the roundabout
  • at reception / at her desk
  • at the next stop
🗂️ on = на поверхности
  • on the wall / on the ceiling / on the floor / on the table
  • on your nose / on your shirt — о пятне
  • on page seven / on an island / on the beach / on the grass

🔍 Одно место — разные предлоги

in — внутри магазинаThere were a lot of people in the shop.
at — ориентир на картеTurn left at the shop.
in — в зданииI'll meet you in the hotel lobby.
at — снаружи, у входаI'll meet you at the entrance.
in — внутри бутылкиThere's some water in the bottle.
on — наклеена снаружиThere's a label on the bottle.
at — человек у двериThere's somebody at the door.
on — табличка на двериThere's a notice on the door.

📋 Списки, которые надо просто знать

inin a line / a row / a queue · in the front row · in a picture / a photo · in a newspaper / a book · in an office / the sales department · in the sky · in the world · in the country (= не в городе)
onon the left / the right · on the ground floor / the second floor · on a map / a menu / a list / a page / a website · on the coast · on a river (Vienna is on the Danube) · on the way home
atat the top / the bottom of the page · at the end of the street · at the front / the back of a building
inThere's a spider in the bath.
atWait for me at the entrance, not inside.
onYour name is on the list, don't worry.
in / onShe's in the kitchen, the note is on the fridge.
in vs at (перед/сзади)in the front / in the back of a car — но at the front / at the back of a building, cinema — и on the front / on the back of an envelope
углыin the corner of a room — но at / on the corner of a street

🏥 in bed / at work — без артикля

inin bed · in hospital · in prison — He's still in bed. · Anna's mother is in hospital.
atat home · at work · at school · at university — I'll be at work until 5.30.
be home / at homeI'll be home all evening. = I'll be at home… но: eat at home — тут at обязателен
события — atat a party / a meeting / a wedding / a conference / a concert
чужой домI was at Helen's last night. · at the doctor's / the hairdresser's
at — событие/адресWe had dinner at the hotel. · There was a robbery at the supermarket.
in — само зданиеAll the rooms in the hotel have air conditioning. · It's cold in Helen's house.
in — городThe Louvre is in Paris. · They live in a village.
at — точка маршрутаDoes this train stop at Oxford? = на станции

🚌 Транспорт: on a bus, but in a car

onon a bus / a train / a plane / a ship — There were too many people on it.
inin a car / a taxi — Laura arrived in a taxi.
onon a bike / a motorbike / a horse — Jane passed me on her bike.
➡️

Движение: to · into · arrive

Куда — и где не нужен предлог вовсе

🛫 to = движение к месту

go / come / travel togo to China · go to work · drive to the airport · return to London · be taken to hospital
существительныеWelcome to our country! · a trip to Paris · a visit to the museum · on my way to work
been toI've been to Italy four times, but never to Rome.
to — движениеThey are going to France. · Can you come to the party?
in / at — положениеThey live in France. · I'll see you at the party.

⚠️ arrive и home

❌
  • arrive to Madrid
  • arrive to the hotel
  • get to home
  • go to home
✅
  • arrive in Madrid / in Spain (город, страна)
  • arrive at the hotel / the airport / the party (здание, событие)
  • get home · arrive home · on the way home
  • go home · come home

Но get to + место работает нормально: They got to the hotel at midnight. · What time did you get to the party?

🚪 into = внутрь

go / get intoI opened the door and went into the room.
fly intoA bird flew into the kitchen through the window.
pay intoMy salary is paid directly into my bank account.
in вместо intoShe got in the car and drove away. = got into the car · I put it back in the envelope.
противоположностьShe got out of the car and went into a shop.
транспортShe got on the bus. · You need to get off at the next station.
🧰

Прочие значения in / on / at и весь by

in the rain · on purpose · at the age of · by chance

🌧️ in

погода и светin the rain · in the sun · in the shade · in the dark · in bad weather
язык, валютаHow do you say it in Russian? · How much is that in dollars?
чувстваThey're in love. · You seem to be in a bad mood.
мнениеIn my opinion, the film wasn't very good.

📺 on

медиаon TV · on the radio · on the phone — I heard it on the radio.
состояниеThat car is on fire! · The drivers are on strike. · I'm on a diet.
намеренноI didn't do it on purpose. = специально
в целомOn the whole, I enjoy my job.
поездкиon holiday · on a trip · on a tour · on a cruise · away on business

📌 Но: go somewhere for a holiday — Steve has gone to France for a holiday.

🌡️ at + число

возрастTracy left school at 16 / at the age of 16.
скоростьThe train was travelling at 120 miles an hour.
температураWater boils at 100 degrees Celsius.
высота, ценаflying at an altitude of 9,000 metres · shares sold at €20 each

⭐ Всё про by

случайноWe met by chance. · by mistake · by accident (без артикля!) — но: on purpose
транспортby car / train / plane / bus / bike / boat — Jess goes to work by bus.
но с уточнением — in/onin my car, in a taxi · on the train, on the 6.45 train не by my car
пешкомDid you come by car or on foot?
by chanceWe met by chance at the airport.
on purposeHe didn't break it on purpose.
by cardCan I pay by card or is it cash only?
by + авторIt's a novel by a Ukrainian writer.
by + разницаPrices went up by almost 20 per cent.
способsend it by post · pay by card / by cheque · do it by hand но: pay cash / in cash
пассивHave you ever been bitten by a dog? · watched by millions
авторa play by Shakespeare · a painting by Picasso
рядомThe light switch is by the door. · Come and sit by me.
разницаHer salary increased by ten per cent. · Carl won the race by five metres.

⚖️ by vs with: The door was opened with a key (инструмент) — The door was opened by somebody (исполнитель).

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. at, on, in или ничего?

1. The film starts _____ 8 o'clock. → at
2. We're meeting _____ Saturday. → on
3. My brother was born _____ 1998. → in
4. I'll call you _____ next week. → — (без предлога)
5. See you _____ Monday morning. → on
6. It's quiet here _____ the evenings. → in
7. What do you do _____ the weekend? → at (AmE: on)
8. He'll be back _____ two hours. → in
9. I don't like driving _____ night. → at
10. We give presents _____ Christmas, but not _____ Christmas Day. → at … on

B. on time или in time? at the end или in the end?

1. The train arrived exactly _____. → on time
2. We got to the airport just _____ for our flight. → in time
3. _____ of the film everybody was crying. → At the end
4. He couldn't decide, but _____ he stayed at home. → in the end
5. I'm leaving _____ of the month. → at the end
6. Please be _____ — the meeting starts at 9 sharp. → on time

C. Место: in, at, on

1. I'll wait for you _____ the bus stop. → at
2. Who's that man _____ the photo? → in
3. Write your name _____ the top of the page. → at
4. Our flat is _____ the third floor. → on
5. The TV is _____ the corner of the room. → in
6. There's a bakery _____ the corner of the street. → at / on
7. She was sitting _____ the front row. → in
8. My sister is _____ university. → at
9. He's still _____ bed. → in
10. We came _____ a taxi, they came _____ the bus. → in … on

D. Найди ошибку

1. We arrived to London at 6. → arrived in London
2. What time did you get to home? → get home
3. Welcome in Germany! → Welcome to Germany!
4. I go to work by my car. → in my car / by car
5. I saw it in the TV. → on TV
6. She did it by purpose. → on purpose
7. Can I pay with card? → pay by card
8. In my opinion this is right — ошибки нет. → верно, in my opinion
