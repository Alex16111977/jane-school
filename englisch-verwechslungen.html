Слова, которые путают

lie или lay · borrow или lend · affect или effect · do или make · except или besides — пары, на которых спотыкаются все

🔄

Глаголы-двойники

Разница часто в том, кто действует и на что

🛏️ lie или lay

ГлаголФормыСмысл
lielie – lay – lain (lying)лежать (сам) — без дополнения
laylay – laid – laid (laying)класть (что-то) — всегда с дополнением
lie (2)lie – lied – liedврать
лежатьI lay on the beach all day yesterday. · The dog is lying in the sun.
кластьShe laid the baby on the bed. · The table has already been laid.
вратьHe lied to me about his age.

🔑 Проверка: если после глагола стоит что (положил что?) — это lay. Если ничего — lie.

⬆️ rise или raise

rise – rose – risen — сам поднимаетсяPrices rose sharply last year. · The sun rises at six.
raise – raised – raised — поднимают что-тоThey raised the prices again. · Raise your hand if you agree.

💰 borrow или lend · steal или rob

borrow — брать у кого-тоCan I borrow your pen? · I borrowed the money from my brother.
lend — давать кому-тоCould you lend me your pen? · He lent me €50.
steal — красть ВЕЩЬSomebody stole my bike. · The painting was stolen in 1990.
rob — грабить МЕСТО или ЧЕЛОВЕКАThey robbed the bank. · I was robbed in the street.

❌ They stole the bank. ✅ They robbed the bank / stole the money from the bank.

🗣️ say · tell · speak · talk

say something (to sb)He said nothing. · What did you say to her?
tell somebody somethingTell me the truth. · She told us a story. — всегда кому
устойчиво с telltell the truth / a lie / a joke / a story / the time / the difference
speak (a language, to sb)She speaks three languages. · Can I speak to the manager?
talk (about, with)We talked about work all evening.

❌ He said me that… → He told me that… / He said to me that…

🎯 Ещё пары

bring / takeBring me those files, please. сюда · Take the rubbish out. отсюда
listen / hearListen to me carefully. намеренно · I heard a noise outside. непроизвольно
watch / see / look atWatch this film · see a friend · look at the picture
remember / remindI remember her face. · Remind me to call her. напомни мне
win / beatWe won the match. что · We beat Poland 2–0. кого
teach / learnShe teaches maths. · I'm learning Spanish. не I teach Spanish о себе
wait / expectI'm waiting for the bus. стою · I'm expecting a parcel. жду поступления
wear / carryShe was wearing a red coat. · He was carrying two bags.
🔤

Похожие слова

Отличаются одной буквой — и значением целиком

✍️ affect или effect

affect — глагол «влиять на»The closure affected many families.
effect — существительное «эффект»The effects of pollution are everywhere.

Мнемоника: Affect = Action (глагол), Effect = End result (существительное). Устойчиво: have an effect on something.

📎 it's или its

it's = it is / it hasIt's eight o'clock already! · It's been two months since I saw him.
its = его/её (притяжательное)The dog buried its bone. · The plane lowered its wheels.

Проверка: подставь it is. Получилось — пиши it's, нет — its без апострофа.

🕓 ago / before · after / afterwards

ago — назад от сегодняShe left school six months ago. всегда с past simple
before — раньше некоего моментаShe washed her hair before she went to the party.
after — предлог/союзHe called me after I had finished work.
afterwards — наречие «потом»They went to the cinema and afterwards they went home.

❌ two years before в значении «два года назад» → two years ago. Но в косвенной речи: two years before.

🛋️ beside или besides

beside = рядом сThe sofa is beside the fireplace.
besides = кроме того, вдобавокBesides buying us dinner, he paid for the tickets.

👤 Пары-прилагательные

alone / lonelyI live alone. факт · I feel lonely. чувство
sensible / sensitivea sensible decision разумное · a sensitive person чувствительный
economic / economicaleconomic crisis экономический · an economical car экономный
historic / historicala historic day исторический = важный · a historical novel про историю
actual / currentthe actual cost фактический · the current situation нынешний
good / wellHe's a good singer. · He sings well. · I don't feel well. здоров

🧳 Пары-существительные

travel / trip / journeytravel — вообще (I love travel) · a business trip — поездка туда-обратно · a long journey — путь
job / worka good job исчисляемое · a lot of work неисчисляемое
price / prizethe price of petrol цена · she won first prize приз
cook / cookerHe's a great cook. человек · We bought a new cooker. плита
receipt / recipeKeep the receipt. чек · a recipe for soup рецепт блюда
customer / client / guestshop customer · lawyer's client · hotel guest

besides · except · but · except for · apart from

Одно значит «вдобавок», остальные — «кроме»

СловоЗначениеПример
besidesвдобавок, к тому жеIt's too late to go out. Besides, it's going to rain.
exceptкроме (в середине предложения)Everyone got a pay rise except James.
butкроме (после all, everyone, nobody…)I cleaned every room but the kitchen.
except forкроме (часто в начале)Except for the guard, the building was empty.
apart fromкроме (= except for, разговорнее)Apart from washing the car, what else have you done?

🔑 Главная ловушка: besides добавляет («и ещё»), а except вычитает («все, кроме»). Одна буква — противоположный смысл.

📝 Сравни в паре

besides = плюсBesides Anna, three other people came.Анна пришла И ещё трое
except = минусEveryone came except Anna.Анна НЕ пришла
except + сущ.The shop is open every day except Sunday.
except for + причина-оговоркаThe essay is good except for a few spelling mistakes.
apart fromApart from the price, I like the flat.
besides = кроме тогоI don't want to go. Besides, I have no money.
except + thatEverything went well except that we arrived late.
🛠️

do или make

Оба переводятся «делать» — но сочетаются по-разному

🔧 DO — работа, действие, обязанность
  • do the housework / the washing-up / the shopping
  • do your homework / an exercise / a test
  • do a job / business with sb / research
  • do your best / your duty / your hair
  • do harm / damage to / good
  • do a crossword / an experiment / a translation
🏗️ MAKE — создавать, производить
  • make a cake / coffee / the beds
  • make a decision / a choice / an effort
  • make a mistake / an excuse / an offer
  • make a noise / a mess / trouble
  • make an appointment / an arrangement / a discovery
  • make money / progress / peace / up your mind

🔑 Грубое правило: make — когда что-то появляется (торт, решение, шум, ошибка). do — когда просто выполняешь действие или работу.

📝 В предложениях

doI have to do the washing-up before I go out.
doShe always does her best at school.
doThe storm did a lot of damage to the roof.
makeSorry, I made a mistake in the calculation.
makeWe need to make a decision before Friday.
makeStop making so much noise — the baby's asleep.
makeHe's made a lot of progress this term.
оба, но разноеdo your hair причесаться · make your bed заправить постель

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. Выбери глагол

1. She _____ on the sofa and fell asleep. (lie/lay) → lay
2. He _____ the papers on the desk. (lie/lay) → laid
3. Can I _____ your charger? → borrow
4. Could you _____ me €20 until Friday? → lend
5. Somebody _____ my phone at the gym. → stole
6. Three men _____ the post office last night. → robbed
7. Prices have _____ again this month. → risen
8. The landlord _____ the rent by 10 %. → raised
9. He _____ me the truth in the end. → told
10. What did she _____ about the plan? → say
11. We _____ Italy 3–1 in the final. → beat
12. _____ me to buy milk on the way home. → Remind

B. affect / effect · it's / its · beside / besides

1. The weather really _____ my mood. → affects
2. The medicine had no _____ at all. → effect
3. _____ raining again — take an umbrella. → It's
4. The company has changed _____ logo. → its
5. Come and sit _____ me. → beside
6. I don't want to go out. _____, I'm broke. → Besides
7. We moved here three years _____. → ago
8. We had dinner and _____ we watched a film. → afterwards

C. do или make?

1. _____ the shopping → do  ·  2. _____ a decision → make
3. _____ your homework → do  ·  4. _____ a mistake → make
5. _____ progress → make  ·  6. _____ the washing-up → do
7. _____ an appointment → make  ·  8. _____ your best → do
9. _____ a noise → make  ·  10. _____ damage to → do
11. _____ an excuse → make  ·  12. _____ research → do

D. Исправь ошибку

1. He said me that he was tired. → He told me / He said to me…
2. They stole the bank last week. → They robbed the bank.
3. Can you borrow me your pen? → Can you lend me your pen? / Can I borrow your pen?
4. I'm learning my sister to swim. → I'm teaching my sister to swim.
5. The dog buried it's bone. → its bone
6. We won them 2–0. → We beat them 2–0.
7. Everyone came besides Tom. → …except Tom.
8. She made her homework. → She did her homework.
