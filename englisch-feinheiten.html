Feinheiten — мелкие правила, которые пропускают

It's the first time I've… · needn't have vs didn't need to · police are · the giraffe · any better · he asked me where…

Зачем эта страница. Учебник Мёрфи на сайте уже разобран — 14 страниц по темам, которых не было. Но при повторной сверке нашлось 26 правил, которые на существующих страницах (present-perfect, modals, nouns, articles, quantifiers, adjectives, questions) не встречаются вообще или упомянуты вскользь. Они собраны здесь: каждое — с 4–6 живыми примерами, тренировкой и quiz.

1. It's the first time I've … — после «это первый раз» идёт Present Perfect

По-русски мы говорим в настоящем или прошедшем: «я первый раз веду машину», «он второй раз опоздал». По-английски после It's the first / second / third time ставится Present Perfect — всегда.

It's the first time I've done it.  ·  It's the second time she's been late.
first timeIt's the first time I have driven on the left. — впервые за рулём слева (не «I drive»)
second timeIt's the second time he has forgotten his keys this week.
third timeThat's the third time the printer has broken down today.
в прошломIt was the first time I had been in a helicopter. — было прошедшее → Past Perfect
только чтоThis is the first time we have met in person.
отрицаниеIt isn't the first time she has changed her mind.

Та же логика после the only time и после превосходной степени: It's the best film I've ever seen.

2. how long → continuous, how many / how much → simple

Оба перфекта отвечают на разные вопросы. Have been -ing — сколько времени длится. Have done — сколько сделано, сколько раз.

как долго → continuousShe has been writing emails all morning.всё утро пишет
сколько → simpleShe has written twelve emails today.написала двенадцать
как долгоHow long have you been learning German?сколько времени учишь
сколькоHow many words have you learned today?сколько слов выучил
как долгоThey have been building that bridge for two years.
сколько разThey have crossed that bridge three times.

Проверка: если в вопросе есть число или «сколько раз» — нужен simple. Если «с каких пор / как долго» — continuous.

3. I'm always losing things — continuous с always = раздражение

Present Continuous с always / forever / constantly не про «сейчас», а про то, что бесит: «вечно ты…». Present Simple в тех же словах — просто факт, без эмоции.

фактI always lose my keys.такая привычка
жалобаI'm always losing my keys!вечно теряю, достало
фактHe criticises everything.
жалобаHe's always criticising everything.
foreverMy neighbour is forever complaining about the bins.
constantlyThe printer is constantly jamming — we need a new one.
про себяI'm always forgetting people's names, sorry.
в прошломShe was always leaving the door open. — тот же приём в Past Continuous

4. used to do ≠ was doing ≠ be used to doing

Три похожие конструкции, которые путают чаще всего.

ФормаЗначениеПример
used to doраньше делал регулярно, сейчас нетI used to smoke, but I gave up in 2015.
was doingделал в конкретный момент в прошломAt eight I was cooking dinner.
be used to doingпривык, для меня это нормальноI'm used to getting up at five now.
used to + infThere used to be a cinema here. — был раньше, теперь нет
вопросDid you use to live in Kyiv? — в вопросе без -d: use to
отрицаниеI didn't use to like olives.
нет настоящего❌ I use to go swimming → ✅ I usually go swimming.
get used toIt took a month to get used to the noise. — процесс привыкания

5. It's a long time since … — как сказать «давно не…»

Русское «я давно не был в театре» переводится тремя способами, и все три надо знать.

It's ... sinceIt's a long time since I went to the theatre.
It's ... sinceIt's two years since we last met.
I haven't ... forI haven't been to the theatre for ages.
The last timeThe last time I saw him was in March.
вопросHow long is it since you had a holiday?

После since здесь идёт Past Simple (since I went), а не перфект.

1. needn't have done vs didn't need to do — разница в том, сделал ли ты это

Две формы переводятся почти одинаково («не нужно было»), но говорят о разном.

needn't have done — сделал зряYou needn't have cooked — we ate on the train.приготовил, а было не нужно
didn't need to do — не делалI didn't need to cook, so I read a book.не нужно было → и не готовил
needn't haveYou needn't have waited for me — I had my own key. — ждал напрасно
needn't haveShe needn't have worried; the results were fine.
didn't need toWe didn't need to book — the place was half empty. — и не бронировали
didn't need toHe didn't need to take a taxi: the bus goes every ten minutes.
needn't (сейчас)You needn't come tomorrow if you're busy. = don't need to / don't have to

mustn't ≠ needn't: You mustn't tell him (нельзя!) против You needn't tell him (можно не говорить).

2. must have / can't have / may have — догадки о прошлом

Модальный + have + V3 = вывод о том, что уже произошло. Степень уверенности задаёт сам модальный глагол.

УверенностьФормаПример
95% даmust haveThe ground is wet — it must have rained.
50/50may / might / could haveShe might have missed the train.
95% нетcan't / couldn't haveHe can't have seen me — I was at home.
must haveNobody answered — they must have gone out.
must have beenYou cycled 90 km? You must have been exhausted.
can't haveShe can't have forgotten — she wrote it in her diary.
may have beenThe letter may have been lost in the post.
might haveI might have left my phone at work — I'll check.
couldn't haveHe couldn't have known about the change; nobody told him.

В прошлом отрицание догадки — can't have, а не «mustn't have»: mustn't = запрет, не вывод.

3. have got to · might as well · it's time you did

Три разговорные конструкции, которые в учебниках обычно теряются.

have got toI've got to go — my train leaves at six. = have to, но живее; чаще в BrE
have got toShe's got to finish the report before Friday.
had got to❌ в прошлом «had got to» почти не говорят → ✅ I had to go.
might as wellThe bus won't come for an hour — we might as well walk. — всё равно нечего терять
might as wellNobody's using the room, so you might as well work there.
it's time + pastIt's time you went to bed. — давно пора; форма прошедшая, смысл про сейчас
it's about timeIt's about time they fixed that lift! — с укором
it's time toIt's time to go. — нейтрально, без упрёка

4. suggest / insist / demand + should (или без него)

После этих глаголов нет to. Есть два варианта: с should или с «голым» инфинитивом (subjunctive).

suggest / insist / demand / recommend / propose that sb (should) do sth
suggestShe suggested that I should apply for the job.  /  She suggested that I apply.
❌ так нельзя❌ She suggested me to apply. ❌ She suggested me applying.
suggest + -ingShe suggested applying early. — без «кому», тогда -ing
insistHe insisted that we stay for dinner.
demandThe staff demanded that the manager apologise. — без -s, это subjunctive
recommendI'd recommend that you book in advance.
it's essentialIt's essential that everyone knows the plan. — та же схема после adj

1. mathematics is — слова на -ics в единственном числе

Выглядят как множественное (есть -s), но глагол ставится в единственном числе.

mathematicsphysicseconomics politicsathleticsgymnastics statistics (наука)news
-ics = ед. ч.Mathematics is not my strongest subject. (не «are»)
-ics = ед. ч.Politics bores him completely.
newsThe news was better than we expected. — news всегда ед. ч.
но statisticsThese statistics are from 2019. — «данные» = мн. ч.
сравниAthletics is popular here.  /  The athletes are here.

2. the police are — существительные-группы с глаголом во множественном

Слово одно, а людей много — в британском английском глагол ставят в множественном числе.

всегда мн. ч.The police are looking for him.police, cattle, people
можно и так, и такThe team are / is playing well.team, staff, family, government, company, audience
policeThe police have arrested two people. ❌ the police has
staffThe staff are not happy about the new rota.
familyMy family are all coming for the weekend. — думаем о людях
family (как единица)This family has lived here since 1890. — думаем о целом
peoplePeople say a lot of things. — people = мн. ч., «persons» почти не бывает
governmentThe government have changed their mind again. (BrE; AmE: has … its)

3. Twenty pounds is — деньги, время и расстояние как одно целое

Если сумма, срок или расстояние мыслятся как одна величина, глагол в единственном числе — хотя число выглядит множественным.

деньгиTwenty pounds is too much for a taxi. ❌ are
времяTwo years is a long time to wait.
расстояниеTen kilometres isn't far by bike.
срокSix months was enough to finish the course.
но реальная мн. ч.Those coins are from 1970. — отдельные предметы

4. the giraffe has … — the + единственное число для «вида в целом»

Чтобы сказать о виде животных, изобретении или инструменте вообще, английский использует the + существительное в единственном числе. Второй вариант — множественное без артикля.

the + ед. ч. (научно, формально)The giraffe has the longest neck of any animal.
мн. ч. без артикля (обычная речь)Giraffes have very long necks.
вид животныхThe tiger is losing its habitat across Asia.
изобретениеWho invented the telephone? — изобретения всегда с the
инструментShe plays the piano and the violin. — музыкальные инструменты с the
❌ a + вид❌ A giraffe has the longest neck of any animal — тут нужен the.
но спортShe plays tennis. — виды спорта без артикля

5. the rich · the French — the + прилагательное = группа людей

the + прилагательное без существительного = все такие люди. Всегда множественное число, без -s.

the rich / the poorthe young / the old the unemployedthe homelessthe injured the Frenchthe Dutchthe Chinese
группаThe rich are getting richer. ❌ the rich is, ❌ the richs
группаThe unemployed need support, not lectures.
народThe Dutch are famously direct.
один человек❌ a French → ✅ a Frenchman / a French person.
-s естьNationalities on -an get -s: two Germans, three Italians, ten Americans.

6. everyone vs every one — пробел меняет смысл

everyone (одно слово) = все людиEveryone enjoyed the party.= everybody
every one (два слова) = каждый изShe read six books and liked every one.каждую отдельно
everyoneEveryone knows the rule. — глагол в ед. ч.
every one ofEvery one of the windows was broken. — перед «of» только раздельно
про вещиI sent ten emails and every one came back.
theirEveryone brought their own laptop. — после everyone обычно their
each vs everyEach student got a certificate. — по отдельности / Every student passed. — все вместе

1. any better / no worse — усилители перед сравнительной степенью

Перед сравнительной степенью ставится не very, а особый набор слов. Русское «намного дешевле» — это much cheaper, никогда «very cheaper».

much / far / a lot / a bit / a little / slightly / any / no + comparative
сильноmuch / far / a lot cheaperнамного дешевле
слегкаa bit / a little / slightly cheaperчуть дешевле
any + comp.Is your headache any better? — хоть немного лучше?
any + comp.I can't wait any longer.
no + comp.The second flat was no bigger than the first. = совсем не больше
no + comp.He's no worse than the others, honestly.
muchThis route is much quicker in the morning.
❌ very❌ very better / very cheaper → ✅ much better / much cheaper.

2. the more … the better · better and better

Две конструкции для роста и зависимости — в русском они звучат совсем иначе, поэтому их редко используют по-английски.

the + comparative … , the + comparative …  ·  comparative and comparative
the … the …The sooner we start, the sooner we finish.
the … the …The more you practise, the easier it gets.
the … the …The more expensive the hotel, the worse the breakfast.
короткий ответ'Shall I invite Anna too?' 'Yes, the more the better.'
andHer English is getting better and better.
andThe queue got longer and longer.
more and moreTravelling is becoming more and more expensive.

3. as … as · the same as · than me / than I am

Три места, где русскоязычные почти всегда ошибаются в предлоге или падеже.

as … asThe film was as good as the book, which is rare.
not as … asToday isn't as cold as yesterday. = less cold
❌ so … asВ утверждении только as … as: ❌ as good so the book.
the same asYour bag is the same as mine. ❌ the same like, ❌ the same that
the same age asShe's the same age as my sister.
than meHe's taller than me. — разговорно, нормально
than I amHe's taller than I am. — формально; ❌ than I (без am)
as … as meShe works as hard as I do / as hard as me.

4. good vs well · hard vs hardly · fast / late

good — прилагательное, well — наречие (и «здоров»). А hardly — вообще не «усиленно», а «почти не».

СловоЧто этоПример
goodприлагательноеHer English is good.
wellнаречие / «здоров»She speaks English well. · I don't feel well.
hardи прил., и наречиеa hard question · she works hard
hardlyпочти неI hardly know him.
wellYou did that very well. ❌ you did that good
well = здоровHe isn't well enough to travel.
hardlyThere was hardly any traffic this morning. = почти никакого
hardly anybodyHardly anybody came to the lecture.
hardly everWe hardly ever eat out these days.
fast / lateHe drives fast and arrives late. — форма та же, что у прилагательного
latelyI haven't seen her lately. = в последнее время, не «поздно»

5. older / elder · oldest / eldest · superlative + in

olderMy brother is older than me. — с than только older
elderMy elder brother lives in Lviv. — только перед словом, только о родне
eldestShe's the eldest of four children. = the oldest, тоже о семье
superlative + inthe longest river in the world · the best restaurant in town — с местами in, не of
superlative + ofthe happiest day of my life · the youngest of the three.
+ present perfectIt's the best book I've ever read. — после превосходной степени перфект

1. He asked me where I lived — порядок слов как в утверждении

В пересказанном вопросе нет вспомогательного do/does/did и нет инверсии. Подлежащее идёт перед глаголом — как в обычном предложении.

❌ Так не говорят
  • He asked me where did I live.
  • She asked what time is it.
  • They asked me do I speak German.
  • I don't know where is he.
✅ Правильно
  • He asked me where I lived.
  • She asked what time it was.
  • They asked me if I spoke German.
  • I don't know where he is.
whereThe officer asked me where I was going.
whyShe asked why I hadn't replied.
how longThey asked how long I had been waiting.
whatHe asked what my job involved.

2. Вопрос без вопросительного слова → if / whether

Если исходный вопрос начинался с глагола (Do you…? Are you…? Have you…?), в пересказе появляется if или whether.

if'Do you smoke?' → She asked me if I smoked.
if'Have you finished?' → He asked if I had finished.
whetherI asked whether the shop was open. — чуть формальнее
whether … orShe asked whether I wanted tea or coffee. — при выборе только whether
whether + toI can't decide whether to go. — перед to только whether, не if

3. Вежливый вопрос: Do you know where … ? / Could you tell me … ?

Тот же порядок слов, что в пересказе — но времена не сдвигаются, потому что говорим о сейчас. Это самый частый способ вежливо спросить.

Do you knowDo you know where the station is? ❌ where is the station
Could you tell meCould you tell me what time the museum opens?
Do you know ifDo you know if there's a chemist near here?
I wonderI wonder why she left so early.
I've no ideaI've no idea how much it costs.
Have you any ideaHave you any idea when they're arriving?

Сравни: прямой вопрос — Where is he?; вежливый — Do you know where he is?. Инверсия остаётся только в первом.

4. Сдвиг времён в пересказе — и когда его нет

Прямая речьПересказ (главный глагол в прошлом)
'I am tired.'He said he was tired.
'I work here.'She said she worked there.
'I saw him.'He said he had seen him. (или: saw)
'I will call.'She said she would call.
'I can help.'He said he could help.
без сдвигаShe said she is a vegetarian. — она и сейчас вегетарианка
без сдвигаHe told me the train leaves at six. — расписание не изменилось
say vs tellHe said that… / He told me that… ❌ he said me
tell sb toThe doctor told me to rest for a week.
ask sb toI asked him to wait outside.
ask sb not toShe asked me not to tell anyone.

Тренировка

Ответ спрятан — кликни по серому прямоугольнику

A. Первый раз / как долго / вечно

1. It's the first time I have flown (fly) business class.
2. That's the third time she has cancelled (cancel) on us.
3. It was the first time we had met (meet) her parents.
4. How long have you been waiting (you / wait)? — Twenty minutes.
5. How many chapters have you read (you / read) so far?
6. He is always losing (always / lose) his gloves — it drives me mad.
7. It's ages since I went to a concert.
8. I haven't seen (not see) them for two years.

B. needn't have / didn't need to

1. You needn't have brought (not / bring) wine — we had plenty. (принёс зря)
2. I didn't need to get up (not / get up) early, so I slept till nine. (и не вставал)
3. She needn't have worried — the exam was easy. (волновалась напрасно)
4. We didn't need to book a table; the place was empty.
5. You mustn't / needn't tell him — it's a secret. (нельзя!)

C. Догадки о прошлом: must / can't / might + have

1. The lights are off. They must have gone to bed.
2. He can't have heard the bell — he was in the shower.
3. I'm not sure where my keys are. I might have left them at work.
4. You walked 30 km? You must have been exhausted.
5. The parcel may have been delivered to the wrong address.

D. Числа, группы, артикли

1. Mathematics is (be) compulsory until sixteen.
2. The police are (be) investigating the case.
3. Fifty pounds is (be) a lot for a shirt.
4. Three weeks isn't (not be) long enough to learn that.
5. The tiger is losing its habitat. (вид в целом)
6. She plays the piano and — tennis. (инструмент / спорт)
7. The rich are (rich / be) getting richer.
8. Every one of the windows was broken. (everyone / every one)

E. Сравнение

1. Is your back any better today?
2. The new flat is no bigger than the old one.
3. This route is much quicker (much / quick) at this hour.
4. The sooner we leave, the better.
5. His German is getting better and better.
6. My phone is the same as yours.
7. She sings well (good) for someone who never practises.
8. There was hardly any snow last winter.
9. My elder sister is a nurse. (elder / older перед словом)
10. It's the best concert I have ever been to.

F. Пересказанные и вежливые вопросы

1. 'Where do you live?' → He asked me where I lived.
2. 'Do you drive?' → She asked me if I drove.
3. 'What time does it start?' → Could you tell me what time it starts?
4. 'Have you finished?' → He asked if I had finished.
5. 'Why is she late?' → I wonder why she is late.
6. I can't decide whether to accept the offer. (if / whether перед to)
7. The doctor told me to rest (rest) for a week.
8. She asked me not to mention (not / mention) it to anyone.
