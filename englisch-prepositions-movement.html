Предлоги движения

across · along · through · past · towards · into / onto / out of — куда и как движется предмет

🚶

Предлоги движения

Куда, откуда и каким путём

Дополнение к странице Prepositions+: там про время и положение (at 5, in the corner), здесь — про движение: сквозь, вдоль, поперёк, мимо, вверх, вниз.

Пары, которые путают

across / through · over / above · under / below · among / between

🌊 across или through?

across — по поверхностиWe swam across the river.с одного берега на другой
through — внутри объёмаWe walked through the forest.внутри, со всех сторон деревья
acrossHe drove across the desert.
throughThe train goes through the Channel Tunnel.

Проверка: если ты внутри (лес, толпа, туннель, город) — through. Если по плоскости (река, площадь, мост, улица) — across.

✈️ over или above? under или below?

over — над и с движением/накрываяThe plane flew over the city. · She put a blanket over the child.
above — просто вышеThere's a lamp above the desk. · 2000 m above sea level.
under — прямо подThe cat is under the table.
below — ниже по уровнюThe temperature fell below zero. · See the note below.

💡 over ещё значит «больше чем»: It costs over €100. А under — «меньше чем»: children under five.

👥 between или among?

between — между двумя (или названными)The table is between the sofa and the TV.чётко названные объекты
among — среди многихHe was standing among the crowd.неопределённая масса
betweenThere's a big difference between the two brothers.
between + списокShe sat between Tom, Anna and me. — названы поимённо, поэтому between
amongThe letter was among the papers on his desk.

🏠 Ещё пары про положение

in front of / oppositeThe car is parked in front of the house. перед · The bank is opposite the post office. напротив, через дорогу
behindThe garden is behind the house.
next to / beside / bySit next to me. · The light switch is by the door.
nearMy school is near my house — five minutes on foot.
againstA man was leaning against the lamp-post.
outside / insideA girl is standing outside the shop.

Движение или положение

Один и тот же предмет — разные предлоги

🏃 Движение
  • go to the office
  • get into the car
  • jump onto the bed
  • walk towards the door
  • fall off the shelf
  • come out of the shop
🧍 Положение
  • be at the office
  • sit in the car
  • lie on the bed
  • stand at the door
  • be on the shelf
  • be in the shop

🔄 Сравни в парах

движениеShe ran into the room.
положениеShe was in the room.
движениеHe climbed onto the roof.
положениеHe was on the roof.
движениеWe drove to Berlin.
положениеWe stayed in Berlin.

⚠️ С arrive движения не бывает: arrive in Berlin (город) / arrive at the station (здание) — никогда arrive to.
⚠️ home без предлога: go home, get home — не go to home.

🚆 Транспорт: on или in?

get on / offавтобус, поезд, самолёт, велосипед — get on the bus, get off at the next stop
get in(to) / out ofмашина, такси — get into the taxi, get out of the car
положениеon the train / on a plane, но in a car / in a taxi

Тренировка

Кликни по серому, чтобы открыть ответ

A. Выбери предлог движения

1. The children climbed _____ the wall to escape. → over
2. She walked quickly _____ the shops without looking in. → past
3. The current is strong — it's dangerous to swim _____ this river. → across
4. She got dressed and ran _____ the stairs to the kitchen. → down
5. I saw a man walking _____ me, smiling. → towards
6. We pushed our way _____ the crowd to see the stage. → through
7. There was no way across the lake, so we walked _____ it. → round / around
8. The boy got _____ bed and went to the window. → out of
9. I put the bags _____ the table, then put the shopping away. → onto / on
10. Go _____ the corner and you'll see the post office on your right. → round

B. Пары-двойники

1. There's a lamp _____ the desk. (выше, без движения) → above
2. The plane flew _____ our house. → over
3. Put the table _____ the sofa and the TV. → between
4. He was standing somewhere _____ the crowd. → among
5. The temperature dropped _____ zero last night. → below
6. The bank is _____ the post office — just cross the road. → opposite
7. A man was leaning _____ the lamp-post. → against
8. The train from England to France goes _____ the Channel Tunnel. → through

C. Движение или положение?

1. She ran _____ the room and slammed the door. → into
2. She was waiting _____ the room. → in
3. We arrived _____ Madrid on Tuesday. → in
4. We arrived _____ the hotel at midnight. → at
5. What time did you get _____ ? (домой) → home (без предлога)
6. Jane passed me _____ her bike. → on
7. Laura arrived _____ a taxi. → in
8. You need to get _____ at the next station. → off
